
# Proof 4: Christ Came Only for the Lost Sheep of Israel

## The Claim

Christ explicitly stated His mission was exclusively to the lost sheep of the house of Israel. He sent His apostles with the same instruction. The apostles went to Europe. Therefore, Europeans are the lost sheep of Israel.

---

## Scriptural Evidence

### Christ's Own Words

> **Matthew 15:24**: "But he answered and said, **I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel.**"

This is an absolute, exclusive statement:
- "I am not sent": His mission
- "but unto": exclusively to
- "the lost sheep": those who were scattered and lost
- "of the house of Israel": the twelve tribes

### The Canaanite Woman (Matthew 15:21-28)

Context: A Canaanite woman begged Christ to heal her daughter.

> v.23: "But he answered her not a word. And his disciples came and besought him, saying, Send her away; for she crieth after us."

> v.24: "But he answered and said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel."

> v.26: "But he answered and said, It is not meet to take the **children's bread**, and to cast it to **dogs**."

Christ made a clear distinction:
- **Children** = Israel (entitled to the bread)
- **Dogs** = Non-Israelites (not entitled)

The woman acknowledged this: "Truth, Lord: yet the dogs eat of the crumbs which fall from their masters' table." She received a crumb of mercy for her faith, but Christ's mission remained unchanged.

### Instructions to the Apostles

> **Matthew 10:5-6**: "These twelve Jesus sent forth, and commanded them, saying, **Go not into the way of the Gentiles**, and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not: But go rather to the **lost sheep of the house of Israel**."

The apostles were forbidden from going to non-Israelites. Their mission was exclusively to Israel.

### After the Resurrection

> **Acts 1:6**: "When they therefore were come together, they asked of him, saying, Lord, wilt thou at this time **restore again the kingdom to Israel**?"

Even after the resurrection, the apostles understood the mission was about Israel. Christ did not correct them, He simply said the timing was not for them to know (v.7).

---

## Where Were the "Lost Sheep"?

### The Northern Kingdom (10 Tribes)

The "lost sheep" refers primarily to the Northern Kingdom of Israel:
- Deported by Assyria in 722-720 BC (2 Kings 17:6)
- Scattered "beyond the river" (Euphrates)
- Became known as Scythians, Cimmerians, and later European peoples

### Why "Lost"?

They were "lost" because:
1. **Geographically scattered**: Removed from the land
2. **Nationally dissolved**: No longer identified as "Israel"
3. **Spiritually divorced**: Hosea 1:9: "ye are not my people"
4. **Identity forgotten**: Blinded to their heritage

Christ came to find and restore these lost sheep.

---

## The Scope of Salvation

### Only for Israel

> **Matthew 1:21**: "And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall **save his people** from their sins."

"His people", not all peoples, but His specific people: Israel.

> **Luke 1:68**: "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath **visited and redeemed his people**."

> **Luke 1:77**: "To give knowledge of salvation unto **his people** by the remission of their sins."

### The New Covenant

> **Jeremiah 31:31**: "Behold, the days come, saith the LORD, that I will make a new covenant with the **house of Israel**, and with the **house of Judah**."

> **Hebrews 8:8**: "For finding fault with them, he saith, Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when I will make a new covenant with the **house of Israel** and with the **house of Judah**."

The New Covenant was promised to, and made with, Israel and Judah. No other peoples are named.

---

## The Apostles Obeyed

After Pentecost, where did the apostles go?

| Apostle | Destination | People Group |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Peter | Anatolia, Rome | Greeks, Romans |
| Paul | Greece, Rome, Spain | Greeks, Romans, Iberians |
| Andrew | Scythia | Scythians (deported Israelites) |
| Thomas | Parthia | Parthians (deported Israelites) |
| James | Stayed in Jerusalem | Scattered tribes visiting feasts |

They went to **European peoples** and to regions where the **deported Israelites** had settled.

They did **not** go to:
- Sub-Saharan Africa
- East Asia
- The Americas
- India (beyond Parthia)

---

## Synthesis

The logic is clear:

1. **Christ said** He came only for the lost sheep of Israel
2. **Christ commanded** the apostles to go only to the lost sheep of Israel
3. **The apostles went** to Europe and Israelite diaspora regions
4. **Christianity took root** in Europe

Therefore: **Europeans are the lost sheep of Israel.**

If this were not true, then either:
- Christ failed His mission, or
- The apostles disobeyed, or
- Christianity spread to the wrong people

None of these are acceptable conclusions.
