
# Proof 7: The Assyrian Deportations

## The Claim

The Assyrians deported the Northern Kingdom of Israel (10 tribes) around 722-720 BC. These deported Israelites became known as Scythians, Cimmerians, and later the Germanic tribes who populated Europe.

---

## Scriptural Evidence

### The Deportation

> **2 Kings 17:6** — "In the ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria took Samaria, and **carried Israel away into Assyria**, and placed them in **Halah** and in **Habor** by the river of **Gozan**, and in the cities of the **Medes**."

> **2 Kings 17:23** — "Until the LORD removed Israel out of his sight, as he had said by all his servants the prophets. So was **Israel carried away out of their own land to Assyria unto this day**."

> **2 Kings 18:11** — "And the king of Assyria did carry away Israel unto Assyria, and put them in Halah and in Habor by the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes."

### The Scale

The Northern Kingdom comprised 10 tribes:
- Reuben, Simeon, Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Issachar, Zebulun, Ephraim, Manasseh
- Plus portions of Levi scattered among them

Population estimates for the Northern Kingdom at the time of deportation: **2-4 million people**.

### They Never Returned

> **2 Kings 17:23** — "So was Israel carried away out of their own land to Assyria **unto this day**."

Unlike Judah (which returned from Babylon 70 years later), the Northern Kingdom never returned to the land. The writer of 2 Kings, writing centuries later, confirms they were still gone.

## Geographic Details

### Where They Were Placed

| Location | Modern Region | Description |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Halah | Northern Iraq | Upper Mesopotamia |
| Habor (Khabur River) | Syria/Iraq border | Tributary of Euphrates |
| Gozan (Tel Halaf) | Northern Syria | Ancient city |
| Cities of the Medes | Northwest Iran | South of Caspian Sea |

This region is exactly where the Scythians and Cimmerians emerged a few decades later.

### Migration Routes

From the deportation zone, there were two primary routes of migration:

**Northern Route (Land):**
Deportation zone → Caucasus Mountains → Black Sea region → Ukraine/Southern Russia → Central and Western Europe

**Western Route (Sea — via Phoenician connections):**
Deportation zone → Phoenician ports → Mediterranean colonies → Iberia, Gaul, Britain

## Assyrian Records

### The Behistun Inscription

The Behistun Inscription of Darius I (c. 520 BC) lists the peoples under Persian rule. In three languages (Old Persian, Elamite, Babylonian), it names the "Sakā" (Sacae) and "Gimirri" (Cimmerians).

Key discovery: The Babylonian version translates "Sakā" as "Gimirri" — they were the same people.

- **Sakā** = from Isaac
- **Gimirri** = the biblical Gomer (also, Israelites were "completed/finished" as a nation — *gamar* in Hebrew)

### Assyrian Annals

Assyrian records refer to the Northern Kingdom as:
- **Bit-Khumri** — "House of Omri" (after Israel's famous king)
- **Bit-Humria** — Same
- **Khumri** — Israelites

The Cimmerians (Gimirri) appear in Assyrian records immediately after the deportations, in the same region.

## What Happened to Them?

### Phase 1: Deportation Zone (722-650 BC)

The Israelites were settled in the deportation zones. They maintained some identity but began to be called by new names:
- Gimirri (Cimmerians)
- Iskuza (Scythians)
- Sakā (Sacae)

### Phase 2: Caucasus & Black Sea (650-300 BC)

The Scythians/Cimmerians moved northward through the Caucasus into the Black Sea steppes. Greek historians began documenting their presence.

### Phase 3: European Migration (300 BC – 500 AD)

Pressure from eastern peoples (Huns, etc.) pushed the Scythian-descended peoples westward:
- Goths → Germany, Spain, Italy
- Vandals → North Africa, Spain
- Franks → France
- Angles/Saxons → England
- Jutes → Denmark
- Various tribes → Scandinavia

### Phase 4: Christian Europe (500 AD onward)

These peoples formed the nations of Christendom and readily converted to Christianity — recognizing their Shepherd's voice.

## The Prophetic Context

### Why the Deportation Happened

> **2 Kings 17:7-18** — Israel sinned against Yahweh, worshipped other gods, and rejected His covenant. Therefore He "removed them out of his sight."

### The Promise of Restoration

Despite the deportation, Yahweh promised to regather Israel:

> **Jeremiah 31:10** — "Hear the word of the LORD, O ye nations, and declare it in the isles afar off, and say, He that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock."

> **Ezekiel 37:21-22** — "Behold, I will take the children of Israel from among the heathen, whither they be gone, and will gather them on every side, and bring them into their own land: And I will make them one nation."

This regathering happened spiritually through Christ and the spread of Christianity to the European nations — the scattered Israelites.

## Synthesis

The Assyrian deportations are:

1. **Historically documented** — Biblical and Assyrian records agree
2. **Geographically traceable** — The deportation zones are known
3. **Linguistically connected** — Sakā = Isaac, Gimirri = Israelites
4. **Chronologically aligned** — Scythians appear exactly when/where expected
5. **Prophetically fulfilled** — Israel scattered, then regathered under Christ

The 10 tribes did not vanish. They migrated to Europe and became the Christian nations.
