
# Proof 19: Moses' Blessing on the Twelve Tribes

## The Claim

In Deuteronomy 33, Moses blessed the twelve tribes before his death. These blessings complement and expand Jacob's prophecies, further identifying the European nations as Israel.

---

## The Text

> **Deuteronomy 33:1** — "And this is the blessing, wherewith Moses the man of God blessed the children of Israel before his death."

Moses blessed the tribes before Israel entered the Promised Land. These blessings would extend through all generations.

---

## The Twelve Blessings

### Reuben (33:6)

> "Let Reuben live, and not die; and let not his men be few."

**Characteristics:**
- A plea for survival
- Concern about diminishing numbers
- Contrast with Jacob's "shall not excel"

**Fulfillment:** France — survived many crises (Revolution, World Wars), population concerns at various points in history.

---

### Judah (33:7)

> "Hear, LORD, the voice of Judah, and bring him unto his people: let his hands be sufficient for him; and be thou an help to him from his enemies."

**Characteristics:**
- Would call upon the Lord
- Unified with his people
- Protected from enemies

**Fulfillment:** The royal line continued through Judah; Christian Europe preserved Judah's messianic legacy.

---

### Levi (33:8-11)

> "Let thy Thummim and thy Urim be with thy holy one... They shall teach Jacob thy judgments, and Israel thy law: they shall put incense before thee, and whole burnt sacrifice upon thine altar."

**Characteristics:**
- Priesthood
- Teachers of the law
- Sacrificial service

**Fulfillment:**
- Originally: Levitical priesthood
- In diaspora: Druids functioned as Levites (see [[21 - Druids Were Levites]])
- In Christianity: The clergy continued teaching God's law

---

### Benjamin (33:12)

> "The beloved of the LORD shall dwell in safety by him; and the LORD shall cover him all the day long, and he shall dwell between his shoulders."

**Characteristics:**
- Beloved of the Lord
- Dwelling in safety
- Under divine protection

**Fulfillment:**
- Benjamin was the "beloved" tribe (smallest, protected)
- Paul (a Benjamite) brought the Gospel to Rome
- Roman Christianity was protected and spread

---

### Joseph (33:13-17)

> "Blessed of the LORD be his land, for the precious things of heaven, for the dew, and for the deep that coucheth beneath, And for the precious fruits brought forth by the sun... **His glory is like the firstling of his bullock, and his horns are like the horns of unicorns: with them he shall push the people together to the ends of the earth**: they are the ten thousands of Ephraim, and they are the thousands of Manasseh."

**Characteristics:**
- Blessed land with abundant resources
- Agricultural wealth
- **Bullock (ox) and unicorn** symbolism
- Would "push peoples to the ends of the earth" — global expansion
- Ephraim: ten thousands (greater)
- Manasseh: thousands (great, but fewer)

**Fulfillment:**
- Britain and America (see [[17 - Ephraim Manasseh]])
- Colonial expansion = "push peoples to the ends of the earth"
- Unicorn on British Royal Arms
- Natural resources of America, Canada, Australia

---

### Zebulun and Issachar (33:18-19)

> "Rejoice, Zebulun, in thy going out; and, Issachar, in thy tents. They shall call the people unto the mountain; there they shall offer sacrifices of righteousness: for they shall suck of the abundance of the seas, and of treasures hid in the sand."

**Characteristics:**
- Zebulun: going out (maritime, trade)
- Issachar: staying home (agricultural)
- "Abundance of the seas" — maritime wealth
- "Treasures hid in the sand" — mineral wealth

**Fulfillment:**
- Netherlands (Zebulun) — maritime trade, "going out"
- Switzerland (Issachar) — stable, "in thy tents"
- Both near each other geographically

---

### Gad (33:20-21)

> "Blessed be he that enlargeth Gad: he dwelleth as a lion, and teareth the arm with the crown of the head. And he provided the first part for himself, because there, in a portion of the lawgiver, was he seated."

**Characteristics:**
- Enlarged territory
- Lion-like (fierce)
- Associated with lawgiving

**Fulfillment:** Germany — enlarged through history (Holy Roman Empire), fierce military tradition, important legal traditions (Roman-Germanic law).

---

### Dan (33:22)

> "Dan is a lion's whelp: he shall leap from Bashan."

**Characteristics:**
- Young lion (aggressive, growing)
- "Leap from Bashan" — migration from a specific location

**Fulfillment:**
- Bashan was in the Golan Heights region
- Dan "leaped" from there to the Mediterranean (Phoenician connections)
- Then leaped again to Europe (Denmark, Ireland)
- Active, mobile tribe

---

### Naphtali (33:23)

> "O Naphtali, satisfied with favour, and full with the blessing of the LORD: possess thou the west and the south."

**Characteristics:**
- Satisfied, blessed
- Would possess west and south

**Fulfillment:** Scandinavian peoples — possessed territories to the west (Iceland, Greenland, Vinland) and south (Norman conquests in Sicily, southern Italy).

---

### Asher (33:24-25)

> "Let Asher be blessed with children; let him be acceptable to his brethren, and let him dip his foot in oil. Thy shoes shall be iron and brass; and as thy days, so shall thy strength be."

**Characteristics:**
- Blessed with descendants
- Acceptable/favored
- "Dip foot in oil" — wealth, oil production?
- Iron and brass shoes — industrial might
- Strength enduring

**Fulfillment:** Belgium/Netherlands region — wealthy, industrial (iron and steel), enduring despite small size.

---

## Simeon — Missing?

Note: Simeon is not mentioned in Deuteronomy 33. This may confirm:
- Simeon was absorbed into Judah (Joshua 19:1)
- Simeon's tribal identity was lost earlier than others
- The prophecy of being "scattered" (Genesis 49:7) was already in effect

## Comparing Jacob and Moses

| Tribe | Jacob's Focus | Moses' Focus |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| Reuben | Instability, lost birthright | Survival, continuation |
| Judah | Kingship, scepter, Shiloh | Relationship with God |
| Levi | Scattered | Priesthood, teaching |
| Benjamin | Wolf, warrior | Beloved, protected |
| Joseph | Fruitfulness, blessing | Resources, expansion |
| Zebulun | Maritime haven | Going out (trade) |
| Issachar | Burden-bearing | Staying home |
| Dan | Serpent, judging | Lion's whelp, leaping |
| Gad | Overcome then triumph | Enlarged, fierce |
| Asher | Royal dainties | Oil, industry, strength |
| Naphtali | Hind, goodly words | Possess west and south |

The prophecies complement each other, providing a fuller picture.

---

## Synthesis

Moses' blessings confirm and expand Jacob's prophecies:

1. **Joseph's dominance** — Most detailed blessing, global expansion
2. **Ephraim > Manasseh** — Ten thousands vs. thousands
3. **Tribal characteristics** — Match European national traits
4. **Geographic fulfillment** — West, south, seas, expansion
5. **Missing Simeon** — Confirms early absorption

The blessings describe what the tribes would become — and only in Europe do we find nations matching these descriptions.
