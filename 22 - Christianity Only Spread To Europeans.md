
# Proof 22: Christianity Only Spread to Europeans

## The Claim

For 2,000 years, Christianity spread only to Europeans and their colonies. No other continent converted until European missionaries brought the Gospel. Europeans are the sheep who recognized their Shepherd's voice.

---

## The Scriptural Basis

> **John 10:27**: "**My sheep hear my voice**, and I know them, and they follow me."

> **John 10:14**: "I am the good shepherd, and **know my sheep**, and am known of mine."

Christ's sheep recognize Him. Those who are not His sheep do not respond.

---

## The Historical Pattern

### Europe: Mass Conversion

| Region | Converted | How |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| Roman Empire | 1st-4th century | Apostolic mission, then Constantine |
| Germanic tribes | 4th-8th century | Missionaries from Roman Christianity |
| Scandinavia | 9th-11th century | Missionaries from Christian Europe |
| Eastern Europe | 9th-14th century | Byzantine and Roman missions |
| Russia | 10th century | Byzantine mission |

Europe became **entirely Christian** within 1,000 years of Christ, without military conquest (in most cases).

### Outside Europe: Minimal Response

| Region | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Middle East | Early Christians, then overwhelmed by Islam |
| North Africa | Early Christians, then overwhelmed by Islam |
| Persia | Small Christian minority, persecuted |
| India | Tiny Thomas Christian community only |
| China | Nestorian missions failed; no lasting presence |
| Japan | Briefly penetrated, then expelled |
| Sub-Saharan Africa | No Christianity until European colonization |
| Americas | No Christianity until European colonization |

---

## The Contrast

### Who Embraced Christianity?

**Europeans:**
- Converted as entire nations
- Built Christian civilization
- Preserved and copied scriptures
- Sent missionaries worldwide
- Defended Christianity against Islam

**Non-Europeans:**
- Required military conquest (in many cases)
- Remained minority religions
- Mixed Christianity with local religions
- Frequently reverted to prior religions
- Did not organically produce Christian civilization

---

## Why This Pattern?

### The Sheep Hear His Voice

Christianity spread to Europe because Europeans are Israelites, the "lost sheep" Christ came to find.

> **Matthew 15:24**: "I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel."

The apostles went to Europe. Europe converted. Because Europe is Israel.

### The New Covenant Was With Israel

> **Jeremiah 31:31**: "I will make a new covenant with the **house of Israel**, and with the **house of Judah**."

> **Hebrews 8:8**: Same promise quoted in the New Testament.

The New Covenant was made with Israel. Europeans received the New Covenant. Europeans are Israel.

---

## Objections Answered

### "Christianity spread to Ethiopia and India"

**Ethiopia:**
- Ethiopian Christianity exists, but:
- Ethiopia has significant Semitic (Israelite?) ancestry
- The Ethiopian eunuch was reading Isaiah: already connected to Israel
- May represent a branch of scattered Israel

**India (Thomas Christians):**
- Tiny minority (1-2% of population)
- Limited to one region (Kerala)
- Did not spread organically
- India as a whole did not convert

These exceptions may prove the rule, Christianity took root where Israelites were present.

### "Christianity required force in Europe too"

- Charlemagne's Saxon wars were political, not typical
- Most of Europe converted through preaching
- Compare to Islam, which spread by conquest everywhere
- European Christianity was organic and self-sustaining

### "Modern missions have converted non-Europeans"

- Modern conversions follow European colonization
- Often mixed with traditional religions (syncretism)
- Frequently revert when European influence withdraws
- 2,000 years of organic Christian civilization exists only in Europe

## The Fulfillment of Prophecy

### Isaiah's Prophecy

> **Isaiah 49:12**: "Behold, these shall come from far: and, lo, these from the **north** and from the **west**; and these from the land of Sinim."

The regathered Israel comes from the north and west, Europe.

### The Isles

> **Isaiah 42:4**: "The **isles** shall wait for his law."

> **Isaiah 49:1**: "Listen, O **isles**, unto me."

The "isles" (coastlands, maritime nations) received the law, Britain, Scandinavia, Mediterranean Europe.

## Christian Civilization

### What Europeans Built

Only Europeans built Christian civilization:
- Cathedrals
- Universities (originally Christian)
- Hospitals (Christian charity)
- Orphanages
- The scientific method (seeking God's order in creation)
- Constitutional government (based on biblical principles)
- Abolition of slavery (Christian conscience)
- Global missions

No other people group produced this organically.

## Synthesis

Christianity spread only to Europeans because:

1. **Christ came for Israel**: Europeans are Israel
2. **The sheep hear His voice**: Europeans recognized Christ
3. **The New Covenant was with Israel**: Europeans received it
4. **Organic growth**: Christianity took root naturally in Europe
5. **Christian civilization**: Only Europeans built it

The pattern of Christianity's spread identifies who Israel is. Israel is Europe.
