
# Proof 88: Stewardship of Creation

## The Claim

Europeans developed the concept of environmental stewardship, caring for creation as God's gift. This reflects Israel's mandate to "tend and keep" the garden.

---

## The Mandate

> **Genesis 2:15**: "And the LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to dress it and to keep it."

Adam (Israel's ancestor) was given stewardship, not exploitation.

---

## European Practice

Europeans developed:
- Conservation movements
- National parks
- Wildlife protection
- Sustainable forestry
- Environmental science

Often motivated by Christian stewardship theology.

---

## Contrast

Other approaches:
- Exploitation without thought
- Nature worship (no stewardship)
- No conservation ethic

Europeans uniquely balanced use and care.

---

## Synthesis

Stewardship of creation reflects Adam/Israel's original calling, tending the earth as God's servants.
