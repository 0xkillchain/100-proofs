
# Proof 36: The Nature of Esau

## The Claim

Esau's character, worldly, impulsive, profane, and ultimately hostile to God's purposes, is reflected in his descendants. Understanding Esau's nature helps identify his descendants today.

---

## Esau's Character

### Profane (Worldly-Minded)

> **Hebrews 12:16**: "Lest there be any fornicator, or **profane person, as Esau**, who for one morsel of meat sold his birthright."

"Profane" (Greek: *bebelos*) = common, unholy, worldly

Esau was:
- Focused on immediate gratification
- Dismissive of spiritual things
- Willing to trade eternal blessing for temporary pleasure

### Despised the Birthright

> **Genesis 25:34**: "Thus **Esau despised his birthright**."

The birthright included:
- Leadership of the family
- Double portion of inheritance
- The covenant promises

Esau considered these worthless compared to a bowl of stew.

### No Place for Repentance

> **Hebrews 12:17**: "For ye know how that afterward, when he would have inherited the blessing, he was rejected: for **he found no place of repentance, though he sought it carefully with tears**."

Even when Esau wanted the blessing:
- He was rejected
- Repentance was not available to him
- His tears didn't change his status

This suggests a fundamental inability to repent, rooted in his nature, not just his choices.

---

## Esau's Choices

### Married Canaanite Women

> **Genesis 26:34-35**: "And Esau was forty years old when he took to wife Judith the daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Bashemath the daughter of Elon the Hittite: Which **were a grief of mind unto Isaac and to Rebekah**."

> **Genesis 28:8-9**: "And Esau seeing that the daughters of Canaan pleased not Isaac his father; Then went Esau unto Ishmael, and took... Mahalath the daughter of Ishmael."

Esau married:
- Hittite women (Canaanites)
- An Ishmaelite woman
- Against his parents' wishes
- Corrupting his lineage

### Planned to Kill Jacob

> **Genesis 27:41**: "And Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing wherewith his father blessed him: and Esau said in his heart, The days of mourning for my father are at hand; then **will I slay my brother Jacob**."

Esau's response to losing the blessing was murder.

---

## Esau's Descendants

### Like Father, Like Sons

The nature of Esau passed to his descendants:
- Worldly, materialistic
- Hostile to Jacob/Israel
- Willing to use violence
- "Perpetual hatred" (Ezekiel 35:5)

### The Edomite Character

Throughout Scripture, Edom displays:
- Enmity toward Israel
- Rejoicing at Israel's troubles
- Violence against Israel
- Pride and arrogance

> **Obadiah 1:3**: "The **pride of thine heart** hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high; that saith in his heart, Who shall bring me down to the ground?"

---

## The "Red" Connection

### Esau = Edom = Red

> **Genesis 25:25**: "And the first came out **red** [*admoni*], all over like an hairy garment; and they called his name **Esau**."

> **Genesis 25:30**: "And Esau said to Jacob, Feed me, I pray thee, with that same **red** pottage; for I am faint: therefore was his name called **Edom** [Red]."

Esau was associated with "red":
- Born red/ruddy
- Named Edom (Red) after the stew
- His territory called Edom

### Symbolism

Red symbolizes:
- Blood (violence)
- Earthliness (Adam from *adamah*: red earth)
- Revolution (red flags of communism)

The "red" nature of Esau suggests earthliness, violence, and bloodshed.

---

## Esau vs. Jacob

### Contrasting Natures

| Esau | Jacob |
|------|-------|
| Profane | Valued the birthright |
| Impulsive | Calculating |
| Hunter (violent) | Shepherd (pastoral) |
| Married Canaanites | Married within the family |
| Hated his brother | Feared but reconciled |
| No repentance available | Wrestled with God, prevailed |

### The Fundamental Difference

Jacob valued spiritual things. Esau did not.

Jacob struggled but prevailed. Esau rejected and was rejected.

Jacob became Israel. Esau became Edom, the enemy of Israel.

---

## The Modern Manifestation

### Identifying Esau Today

If Esau's nature continues in his descendants, we should see:
- Materialism (valuing earthly things over spiritual)
- Hostility to Christians/Europeans
- Perpetual hatred of Jacob's descendants
- Control through commerce and finance
- Revolutionary spirit ("break his yoke")

These characteristics appear in those who claim Jacob's inheritance but display Esau's nature.

---

## Synthesis

Esau's nature proves:

1. **Profane character**: Worldly, not spiritual
2. **Despised the birthright**: Rejected covenant promises
3. **No repentance**: Fundamental inability
4. **Corrupted lineage**: Married Canaanites
5. **Perpetual enmity**: Hatred passed to descendants

Esau's nature identifies his descendants, those who display the same profane, hostile, materialistic character.
