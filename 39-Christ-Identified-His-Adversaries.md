
# Proof 39: Christ Identified His Adversaries

## The Claim

Christ directly told certain religious leaders their identity: they were not Abraham's true children but children of the devil. He identified them by their lineage, not merely their behavior.

---

## The Key Passage: John 8

### The Setting

Christ was teaching in the Temple. Certain religious leaders challenged Him.

### The Dialogue

> **v.33** — "They answered him, We be **Abraham's seed**, and were never in bondage to any man."

They claimed descent from Abraham. But "never in bondage" is odd — Israel was in bondage in Egypt, Babylon, etc. These may have been Edomites, who were never in those captivities.

> **v.37** — "I know that ye are **Abraham's seed**; but ye seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you."

Christ acknowledged they were "Abraham's seed" — but Abraham had multiple lines of descent:
- Isaac → Jacob → Israel (the promised line)
- Isaac → Esau → Edom
- Ishmael → Ishmaelites
- Keturah's sons

Being "Abraham's seed" doesn't mean being the promised seed.

### The Critical Distinction

> **v.39** — "They answered and said unto him, Abraham is our father. Jesus saith unto them, **If ye were Abraham's children, ye would do the works of Abraham**."

"Seed" (*sperma*) vs. "Children" (*tekna*):
- **Seed** = Physical descent (they had this)
- **Children** = True heirs (they lacked this)

They were Abraham's seed through Esau, but not Abraham's children through Jacob.

### Their True Father

> **v.41** — "Ye do the deeds of **your father**."

> **v.44** — "Ye are of **your father the devil**, and the lusts of your father ye will do. He was a **murderer from the beginning**, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him."

Christ identified their father as:
- The devil
- A murderer from the beginning
- A liar

"Murderer from the beginning" points to Cain, who murdered Abel. These religious leaders were descendants of Cain's line.

### They Cannot Hear

> **v.43** — "Why do ye not understand my speech? even because **ye cannot hear my word**."

> **v.47** — "He that is of God heareth God's words: **ye therefore hear them not, because ye are not of God**."

They could not hear Christ because:
- They were not of God
- They were not His sheep
- Their nature prevented understanding

---

## Other Passages

### Matthew 23: Woe to the Pharisees

> **v.33** — "Ye **serpents**, ye **generation of vipers**, how can ye escape the damnation of hell?"

"Generation of vipers" = offspring of snakes = seed of the serpent.

> **v.35** — "That upon you may come all the righteous blood shed upon the earth, **from the blood of righteous Abel**..."

Why would they be responsible for Abel's blood? Because they were descendants of Abel's murderer — Cain.

### Matthew 12:34

> "O **generation of vipers**, how can ye, being evil, speak good things?"

Again, "offspring of snakes" — identifying their lineage.

### John 10:26

> "But **ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep**, as I said unto you."

They don't believe because they are not Christ's sheep. It's a matter of nature, not just choice.

---

## The Parable of the Tares

### Matthew 13:24-30, 36-43

> "The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a man which sowed good seed in his field: But while men slept, **his enemy came and sowed tares among the wheat**."

Christ's interpretation:

> "The field is the world; **the good seed are the children of the kingdom**; but **the tares are the children of the wicked one**; The enemy that sowed them is **the devil**."

Two seeds:
1. Children of the kingdom — planted by Christ
2. Children of the wicked one — planted by the devil

These grow together until harvest. The tares (children of the devil) are then gathered and burned.

---

## The Significance

### Not About Behavior Alone

Christ wasn't simply saying "you're acting like the devil." He identified their actual lineage:
- They were children of the devil
- They descended from a murderer
- They were serpents, vipers
- They were responsible for Abel's blood

### Lineage Determines Nature

> **John 8:44** — "The lusts of your father **ye will do**."

Their lineage determined their nature. They "will do" (inevitably, not just possibly) their father's lusts.

### Christ Knew Who They Were

Christ:
- Knew they were Abraham's seed (through Esau)
- Knew they were not Abraham's true children
- Knew they were children of the devil
- Identified them publicly

---

## Modern Application

### Who Are Christ's Adversaries Today?

Those who:
- Claim Abraham's inheritance but are not true Israel
- Display the nature of their father (lying, murder, opposition to Christ)
- Cannot hear Christ's word
- Are "serpents" and "vipers"

The same adversaries Christ faced continue today — His enemies are still Esau's and Cain's descendants.

---

## Synthesis

Christ identified His adversaries as:

1. **Abraham's seed, not children** — Physical descent without spiritual heritage
2. **Children of the devil** — Satan/Cain as their father
3. **Murderers by nature** — From a "murderer from the beginning"
4. **Serpents and vipers** — Seed of the serpent
5. **Unable to hear** — Nature prevents understanding

Christ's words reveal who His enemies were — and who they continue to be.
