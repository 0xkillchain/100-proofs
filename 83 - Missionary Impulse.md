
# Proof 83: The Missionary Impulse

## The Claim

Europeans uniquely carried the Gospel to all nations. This missionary impulse fulfilled Israel's calling to be "a light to the nations" and to bring blessing to "all families of the earth."

---

## The Great Commission

> **Matthew 28:19** — "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations."

Europeans fulfilled this:
- Missionaries to every continent
- Bible translated into hundreds of languages
- Churches planted globally
- Christianity spread worldwide

---

## No Other People Did This

| People | Missionary Activity |
|--------|---------------------|
| Europeans | Global, sustained, centuries-long |
| Others | Minimal until European influence |

Only Europeans carried the Gospel globally.

---

## Israel's Calling

> **Isaiah 49:6** — "I will also give thee for a light to the Gentiles [nations], that thou mayest be my salvation unto the end of the earth."

Israel was called to be a light. Europeans became that light.

---

## Synthesis

The missionary impulse proves Europeans are Israel — the people called to bring God's light to the nations.
