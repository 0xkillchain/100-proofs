
# Proof 89: Marriage and Family

## The Claim

European Christian civilization upheld monogamous marriage and the nuclear family as the social foundation. This reflects Israel's covenant understanding of marriage as sacred bond.

---

## The Model

European marriage:
- One man, one woman
- Lifelong commitment
- Foundation of society
- Children raised by parents
- Sanctified by church

---

## The Biblical Pattern

> **Genesis 2:24**: "Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh."

> **Malachi 2:14**: "The LORD hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth."

Marriage as covenant, not contract.

---

## Contrast

Other cultures practiced:
- Polygamy
- Concubinage
- Arranged child marriage
- Easy divorce

Europeans upheld a higher standard.

---

## Synthesis

European marriage norms reflect Israel's covenant understanding, one flesh, lifelong, sacred.
