
# Proof 90: Education for All

## The Claim

Europeans developed universal education — the idea that all children should learn to read. This stemmed from the Protestant conviction that everyone should read Scripture.

---

## The Development

- Luther: Schools for all children
- Reformation: Literacy campaigns
- Public schools: European invention
- Universities: Originally Christian foundations

---

## The Motivation

> **Deuteronomy 6:7** — "And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children."

Israel was commanded to educate. Europeans obeyed.

The Reformation demanded Scripture access → required literacy → built schools.

---

## Contrast

Other civilizations:
- Education for elites only
- No mass literacy goal
- No universal schooling concept

Europeans uniquely educated the masses.

---

## Synthesis

Universal education reflects Israel's command to teach children God's word — a distinctly Israelite value.
