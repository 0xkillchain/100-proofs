
# Proof 57: The Two Witnesses

## The Claim

The "two witnesses" of Revelation 11 can be interpreted as representing the two houses of Israel — Judah and Israel (the ten tribes) — or as the Old and New Testaments preserved by European Christians. Either interpretation points to Israel's identity.

---

## The Text

### Revelation 11:3-4

> "And I will give power unto my **two witnesses**, and they shall prophesy a thousand two hundred and threescore days, clothed in sackcloth. These are the **two olive trees**, and the **two candlesticks** standing before the God of the earth."

### Revelation 11:7-12

> "And when they shall have finished their testimony, the beast that ascendeth out of the bottomless pit shall make war against them, and shall overcome them, and kill them... And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet."

---

## Interpretation 1: The Two Houses

### Judah and Israel

The "two witnesses" may represent:
- **House of Judah** — The southern kingdom
- **House of Israel** — The ten northern tribes

Both houses:
- Witnessed to God's truth
- Were persecuted ("killed")
- Will be resurrected/restored

### Zechariah's Vision

> **Zechariah 4:11-14** — "What are these **two olive trees** upon the right side of the candlestick and upon the left side thereof?... These are the **two anointed ones**, that stand by the Lord of the whole earth."

The two olive trees = two anointed witnesses. In Zechariah's time: Zerubbabel (ruler) and Joshua (priest) — representing kingly and priestly functions.

In prophecy: The two houses of Israel, both anointed for witness.

---

## Interpretation 2: Old and New Testaments

### The Two Covenants

The "two witnesses" may represent:
- **Old Testament** — The Law and Prophets
- **New Testament** — The Gospel and Apostolic witness

Both testaments:
- Witness to God's truth
- Were suppressed (by papal authority)
- Were "killed" (forbidden to common people)
- Were resurrected (Reformation, printing)

### European Preservation

Both testaments were preserved by:
- European Christians
- In European languages
- For European peoples

The witnesses testified in Israel (Europe).

---

## The Persecution

### Revelation 11:7

> "The beast... shall make war against them, and shall overcome them, and **kill them**."

If the witnesses are Israel:
- Persecution under Rome
- Persecution under the papacy
- Spiritual "death" during apostasy

If the witnesses are Scripture:
- Bibles burned
- Translation forbidden
- Scripture suppressed

Both interpretations point to European Christian experience.

---

## The Resurrection

### Revelation 11:11

> "And after three days and an half the **Spirit of life from God entered into them**, and they stood upon their feet."

If Israel:
- Restoration of identity
- Revival of faith
- Future awakening

If Scripture:
- The Reformation
- Mass printing of Bibles
- Scripture available to all

The "resurrection" of the witnesses happened in Europe.

---

## The 1,260 Days

### Revelation 11:3

> "They shall prophesy **a thousand two hundred and threescore days**, clothed in sackcloth."

1,260 days = 3.5 prophetic years

Using the day-year principle:
- 1,260 years of testimony "in sackcloth"
- Persecution era
- From ~538 AD (papal supremacy) to ~1798 AD (papal captivity)?

This period corresponds to European Christian history under papal authority.

---

## The Location

### Revelation 11:8

> "And their dead bodies shall lie in the street of **the great city**, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, **where also our Lord was crucified**."

"Where our Lord was crucified":
- Literally: Jerusalem
- Spiritually: Any place of persecution

If Jerusalem, the witnesses relate to Israel.
If Europe (under corrupt religion), the witnesses are European Christians/Scripture.

---

## Synthesis

The two witnesses prove:

1. **Two houses interpretation** — Judah and Israel witnessing
2. **Two testaments interpretation** — Old and New preserved
3. **European fulfillment** — Persecution and resurrection in Europe
4. **1,260 years** — Matches European Christian history
5. **Israel connection** — Either way, points to Israel

The two witnesses testify in and through Israel — the European Christians.
