
# Proof 96: Exploration and Discovery

## The Claim

Europeans explored and mapped the entire world. This fulfills the prophecy that Israel would "spread abroad to the west, and to the east, and to the north, and to the south."

---

## The Achievement

Europeans:
- Circumnavigated the globe
- Mapped all continents
- Explored polar regions
- Charted the seas
- Discovered new lands

No other people systematically explored the entire earth.

---

## The Prophecy

> **Genesis 28:14**: "Thou shalt spread abroad to the west, and to the east, and to the north, and to the south."

> **Isaiah 54:3**: "Thy seed shall inherit the Gentiles [nations], and make the desolate cities to be inhabited."

---

## The Motivation

European explorers sought:
- New lands for settlement
- Trade routes
- Missionary opportunities
- Scientific knowledge

Often explicitly Christian in motivation.

---

## Synthesis

European exploration fulfills Israel's prophetic destiny, spreading to all directions, filling the earth.
