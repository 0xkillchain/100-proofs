
# Proof 17: The Blessing on Ephraim and Manasseh

## The Claim

Jacob blessed Joseph's sons Ephraim and Manasseh with a specific prophecy: Manasseh would become a great nation, while Ephraim would become a multitude of nations. Only Britain (Ephraim) and America (Manasseh) have fulfilled these prophecies.

---

## The Blessing

### Genesis 48:13-20

Joseph brought his two sons to Jacob for blessing. He positioned:
- **Manasseh** (firstborn) at Jacob's right hand
- **Ephraim** (younger) at Jacob's left hand

But Jacob crossed his hands:

> **v.14** — "And Israel stretched out his right hand, and laid it upon **Ephraim's head, who was the younger**, and his left hand upon Manasseh's head, **guiding his hands wittingly**; for Manasseh was the firstborn."

> **v.19** — "And his father refused, and said, I know it, my son, I know it: he also shall become a people, and he also shall be great: but truly **his younger brother shall be greater than he**, and his seed shall become **a multitude of nations**."

> **v.20** — "In thee shall Israel bless, saying, God make thee as Ephraim and as Manasseh."

### The Prophecy

| Son | Position | Prophecy |
|-----|----------|----------|
| **Manasseh** (firstborn) | Left hand (lesser blessing) | "Shall become a people... shall be great" — **A great nation** |
| **Ephraim** (younger) | Right hand (greater blessing) | "A multitude of nations" — **A company/empire of nations** |

---

## Fulfillment in Britain and America

### Ephraim = Britain

Britain became "a multitude of nations":

**The British Empire (at peak, ~1920):**
- United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales, N. Ireland)
- Canada
- Australia
- New Zealand
- India
- South Africa
- Egypt (protectorate)
- Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda
- Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia
- Jamaica, Bahamas, Barbados
- And dozens more

**The Commonwealth:**
- Still includes 56 nations
- Queen Elizabeth II was head of state for 15 countries
- Cultural, legal, linguistic unity across the globe

No other entity in history matches "a multitude of nations" like the British Empire/Commonwealth.

### Manasseh = America

America became "a great nation" (singular):

**The United States:**
- Single greatest nation in history
- Largest economy
- Most powerful military
- Cultural superpower
- Global influence

America is **one nation**, but the greatest single nation ever — exactly as prophesied for Manasseh.

### The Relationship

The prophecy also predicted:
- **Ephraim greater than Manasseh** — Britain was the older brother, the mother country
- **But Manasseh would be great** — America would surpass Britain in power (post-WWII)
- **Yet Ephraim's seed = multitude** — Britain's legacy is the Commonwealth

The pattern matches:
- Britain colonized America (older brother)
- America grew greater in power (younger becoming greater)
- But Britain's legacy is the "multitude of nations"

## Joseph's Double Portion

### Why Joseph's Sons?

Joseph received the birthright blessing (1 Chronicles 5:1-2), which included:
- Double portion of inheritance
- The blessing of the firstborn

Because of Reuben's sin, the birthright went to Joseph. But Joseph's blessing was divided between his two sons — hence two tribes from Joseph.

### Ephraim and Manasseh as Half-Tribes

In Israel's tribal system:
- Joseph's inheritance was split
- Ephraim and Manasseh each received territory
- Together they represented Joseph's double portion

## Jacob's Prophecy in Genesis 49

> **Genesis 49:22-26** — "Joseph is a fruitful bough, even a fruitful bough by a well; whose branches run over the wall: The archers have sorely grieved him, and shot at him, and hated him: But his bow abode in strength, and the arms of his hands were made strong by the hands of the mighty God of Jacob... The blessings of thy father have prevailed above the blessings of my progenitors unto the utmost bound of the everlasting hills: they shall be on the head of Joseph."

Key elements:
- **Fruitful bough** — Prosperous, growing
- **Branches run over the wall** — Expansion beyond boundaries (colonization)
- **Archers have grieved him** — Enemies, wars
- **Bow abode in strength** — Military power
- **Utmost bound of the everlasting hills** — Distant lands, global reach

This describes colonial expansion — "branches running over the wall" to the "utmost bound."

## Moses' Prophecy in Deuteronomy 33

> **Deuteronomy 33:13-17** — "Blessed of the LORD be his land, for the precious things of heaven... And for the precious things of the earth and fulness thereof... His glory is like the firstling of his bullock, and his horns are like the horns of unicorns: with them he shall **push the people together to the ends of the earth**: they are the **ten thousands of Ephraim**, and they are the **thousands of Manasseh**."

Key elements:
- **Blessed land** — Abundant natural resources (America, Australia, etc.)
- **Push the people to the ends of the earth** — Global colonization/influence
- **Ten thousands of Ephraim** — Greater population/reach
- **Thousands of Manasseh** — Great, but not as numerous

Britain colonized globally; America became the single great nation. The prophecy distinguishes their roles.

## Objections Answered

### "Why Not 13 Tribes?"

With Ephraim and Manasseh counted separately, there are 13 tribes:
- The original 12 minus Levi (no territory) = 11
- Plus Ephraim and Manasseh = 13

But commonly counted as 12 by grouping Joseph's sons or excluding Levi.

### "Couldn't This Be Coincidence?"

The specificity makes coincidence implausible:
- **Two brother nations** — Britain and America are uniquely related
- **Multitude vs. single** — Britain = empire; America = one nation
- **Greater and great** — Both must be great, but one greater
- **Timing** — Had to emerge after Christ, as Christian nations

No other pair of nations in history fits this prophecy.

## Synthesis

The Ephraim-Manasseh prophecy proves:

1. **Specificity** — Two distinct entities: multitude vs. single nation
2. **Relationship** — Brother nations, one from the other
3. **Unique fulfillment** — Only Britain and America match
4. **Christian identity** — Both are historically Christian nations
5. **Timing** — Fulfilled in the modern era, as prophesied

Ephraim = Britain (multitude of nations)
Manasseh = America (great single nation)

This is one of the most specific and verifiable prophecies identifying Israel in Europe.
