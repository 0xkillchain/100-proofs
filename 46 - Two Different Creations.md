
# Proof 46: Two Different Creations

## The Claim

Genesis 1 and Genesis 2 describe different creation events. Genesis 1 describes the creation of humans in general on Day 6. Genesis 2 describes the specific formation of Adam — a separate, later event for a covenant purpose.

---

## The Textual Differences

### Genesis 1:26-27

> "And God said, Let us make **man** in our image... So God **created** man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; **male and female created he them**."

### Genesis 2:7-22

> "And the LORD God **formed** man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life... And the LORD God said, It is not good that the man should be alone... And the rib, which the LORD God had taken from man, made he a woman."

---

## Key Differences

| Feature | Genesis 1 | Genesis 2 |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Name for God | Elohim (God) | Yahweh Elohim (LORD God) |
| Action | Created (*bara*) | Formed (*yatsar*) |
| Humanity | Male and female together | Adam first, Eve later |
| Method | By command | By hands-on forming |
| Breath | Not mentioned | *Neshamah* breathed directly |
| Location | The earth generally | Placed in Eden specifically |

---

## Different Hebrew Words

### *Bara* vs. *Yatsar*

**Bara (בָּרָא):**
- To create from nothing or to bring into being
- Divine creative act
- Used for cosmic, grand-scale creation

**Yatsar (יָצַר):**
- To form, shape, fashion
- Like a potter with clay
- Personal, hands-on crafting

Genesis 1 uses *bara* — cosmic creation
Genesis 2 uses *yatsar* — personal formation

---

## Two Populations?

### Evidence

**Cain's wife:**
> **Genesis 4:17** — "And Cain knew his wife; and she conceived."

Where did Cain's wife come from if only Adam, Eve, and their children existed?

**Cain's fear:**
> **Genesis 4:14** — "And it shall come to pass, that **every one that findeth me shall slay me**."

Who would find and slay Cain if only his family existed?

**The land of Nod:**
> **Genesis 4:16** — "And Cain... dwelt in the land of Nod."

Nod had a population — who were they?

### The Answer

If Genesis 1 describes a general creation of humans, and Genesis 2 describes Adam's specific formation, then:
- Other humans existed from Day 6
- Cain married one of them
- Cain feared these other peoples
- Nod was already populated

---

## Adam's Special Purpose

### Why a Second Creation?

Adam was formed specifically to:
- Bear God's image in a unique way
- Receive the *neshamah* (divine breath)
- Be the covenant head
- Father the Israelite race
- Be the ancestor of Christ

### The Line of Promise

The promise (Genesis 3:15) runs through:
> Adam → Seth → Noah → Shem → Abraham → Isaac → Jacob (Israel) → Judah → David → Christ

This is a specific lineage from Adam — not all humanity.

---

## Supporting Scripture

### 1 Corinthians 15:45-47

> "The **first man Adam** was made a living soul; the **last Adam** was made a quickening spirit... The first man is of the earth, earthy: the second man is the Lord from heaven."

Adam is "the first man" in a specific sense — the first of his line, the Adamic race.

### Luke 3:38

> "Which was the son of Enos, which was the son of Seth, which was the son of **Adam, which was the son of God**."

Adam is uniquely called "son of God" — a special relationship.

### Acts 17:26

> "And hath made of **one blood** all nations of men for to dwell on all the face of the earth."

"One blood" may refer to Adam's race — the nations that came from Adam, not every human on earth.

---

## Implications

### Israel from Adam

If Adam was specially created:
- His descendants are a distinct race
- Israel (from Adam through Abraham) has unique status
- The covenant applies to this lineage
- Redemption is for Adam's race

### Others

Those not from Adam:
- May have been created in Genesis 1
- Are not part of the covenant
- Are not redeemable by Adam's kinsman
- Are "strangers from the covenants of promise"

---

## Objections Answered

### "Two accounts of the same event"

The differences are too significant:
- Different Hebrew words
- Different sequences (male/female together vs. separate)
- Different methods (command vs. formation)
- Different outcomes (general vs. in Eden)

### "All humans are from Adam"

This assumes what it seeks to prove. The text allows for (and some evidence suggests) pre-Adamic peoples.

---

## Synthesis

Two creations explains:

1. **Textual differences** — Genesis 1 vs. 2 are distinct
2. **Cain's world** — Other people existed
3. **Adam's uniqueness** — *Neshamah*, covenant, lineage
4. **Israel's distinctiveness** — From Adam specifically
5. **Redemption's scope** — For Adam's race

Adam's special creation establishes Israel's unique identity as God's covenant people.
