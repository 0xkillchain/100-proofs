
# Proof 23: The New Covenant Is With the Same People

## The Claim

The New Covenant was promised specifically to "the house of Israel and the house of Judah" — the same people as the Old Covenant. If Europeans received the New Covenant, they must be Israel and Judah.

---

## The Promise

### Jeremiah 31:31-34

> "Behold, the days come, saith the LORD, that I will make a **new covenant** with the **house of Israel**, and with the **house of Judah**: Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day that I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt; which my covenant they brake, although I was an husband unto them, saith the LORD: But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the LORD, **I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts**; and will be their God, and they shall be my people."

### Key Elements

1. **New covenant** — Different from Sinai
2. **Same people** — "House of Israel and house of Judah"
3. **Same relationship** — "I was an husband unto them"
4. **Internalized law** — Written on hearts, not stone

---

## Quoted in the New Testament

### Hebrews 8:8-12

The author of Hebrews quotes Jeremiah 31 in full:

> "For finding fault with them, he saith, Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when I will make a **new covenant** with the **house of Israel** and with the **house of Judah**..."

This confirms:
- The New Covenant is what Jeremiah prophesied
- It is with the **same people** — Israel and Judah
- No other peoples are added to the covenant

### Hebrews 8:13

> "In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old."

The Old Covenant is obsolete. The New Covenant replaces it — but with the same covenant people.

---

## Who Received the New Covenant?

### The Logic

1. The New Covenant was promised to Israel and Judah
2. The New Covenant was established through Christ
3. Europeans received and embraced the New Covenant
4. Therefore, Europeans are Israel and Judah

### The Recipients

The New Testament was written to:
- Romans, Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, etc. — all European
- "The twelve tribes scattered abroad" (James 1:1)
- "Strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia..." (1 Peter 1:1)

These are the people who entered the New Covenant.

---

## The Marriage Analogy

### Old Testament Background

Yahweh was "married" to Israel:

> **Jeremiah 31:32** — "Although **I was an husband unto them**, saith the LORD."

> **Isaiah 54:5** — "For thy Maker is thine **husband**; the LORD of hosts is his name."

> **Hosea 2:16** — "Thou shalt call me **Ishi** [my husband]."

### The Divorce

Because of Israel's unfaithfulness:

> **Jeremiah 3:8** — "And I saw, when for all the causes whereby backsliding Israel committed adultery **I had put her away, and given her a bill of divorce**."

Israel was divorced — legally separated from Yahweh.

### The Remarriage

Under Mosaic law, a divorced woman could not remarry her first husband (Deuteronomy 24:1-4). How could Yahweh remarry Israel?

**The solution:** Death dissolves marriage. Christ died, dissolving the old marriage/covenant, allowing remarriage under a New Covenant.

> **Romans 7:1-4** — "Wherefore, my brethren, ye also are become **dead to the law by the body of Christ**; that ye should be **married to another**, even to him who is raised from the dead."

Christ died so Israel could be remarried to Him under the New Covenant.

---

## The Scope of the Covenant

### Not Universal

The New Covenant was not made with "all humanity":

> **Hebrews 8:8** — "I will make a new covenant with the **house of Israel** and with the **house of Judah**."

No other nations are named. No universal expansion is indicated in the covenant promise itself.

### Gentiles = Nations of Israel

When Paul speaks of "Gentiles" receiving salvation, the Greek word is *ethnos* (nations). In context, these are the "nations" that Abraham would father — the nations of scattered Israel.

> **Romans 9:24-26** — "Even us, whom he hath called, not of the Jews only, but also of the **Gentiles** [nations]? As he saith also in Osee [Hosea], I will call them **my people, which were not my people**."

Paul quotes Hosea, which was written specifically about the Northern Kingdom of Israel — not about non-Israelites.

---

## Hosea's Prophecy

### The Context

Hosea prophesied to the Northern Kingdom before its deportation:

> **Hosea 1:9-10** — "Then said God, Call his name **Loammi** [not my people]: for ye are **not my people**, and I will not be your God. Yet the number of the children of Israel shall be as the sand of the sea... and it shall come to pass, that in the place where it was said unto them, **Ye are not my people**, there it shall be said unto them, **Ye are the sons of the living God**."

This was fulfilled when scattered Israel (Europeans) received the Gospel and were again called "sons of the living God."

### Paul's Application

Paul explicitly applies this to his audience:

> **Romans 9:25-26** — Quotes Hosea, applying it to the nations receiving the Gospel.

> **1 Peter 2:10** — "Which in time past were **not a people**, but are now **the people of God**: which had **not obtained mercy**, but now have **obtained mercy**."

Peter quotes Hosea to his European audience — because they are the scattered Israelites Hosea prophesied about.

---

## Synthesis

The New Covenant proves European-Israelite identity because:

1. **The promise was specific** — To Israel and Judah only
2. **The New Testament confirms this** — Hebrews 8 quotes Jeremiah 31
3. **Europeans received it** — Christianity spread to Europe
4. **Hosea's prophecy applies** — "Not my people" became "my people"
5. **The marriage is restored** — Christ died to remarry Israel

The New Covenant was made with Israel. Europeans are in the New Covenant. Europeans are Israel.
