
# Proof 8: The Scythians Were Israelites

## The Claim

The Scythians appeared in history at the exact time and place where the Israelites were deported. Greek historians called them "Sacae" (from Isaac). They later became the Germanic tribes who populated Europe.

---

## Historical Emergence

### Timing

| Event | Date |
|-------|------|
| Assyrian deportation of Israel | 722-720 BC |
| Scythians first appear in historical records | ~700 BC |
| Cimmerians first appear in historical records | ~700 BC |

This is not coincidence. The Scythians emerged in history exactly when the Israelites were deported — within a single generation.

### Location

The Scythians appeared:
- North of the Caucasus Mountains
- Around the Black Sea
- In the steppes of modern Ukraine and Southern Russia

This is directly north of the deportation zone (the cities of the Medes, south of the Caspian Sea). The migration route through the Caucasus passes leads exactly to Scythian territory.

---

## The Names

### Sacae / Sakā

The Persians called the Scythians "Sakā" (plural: Sakae).

Hebrew: **Yitzhak** (יצחק) — Isaac
- Dropping the "Y" prefix (common in Hebrew names): Itzhak → Sak
- The Assyrians would have heard "house of Isaac" as "Bit-Sak" or similar
- Persian "Sakā" = "people of Isaac"

> **Amos 7:16** — "Thou sayest, Prophesy not against Israel, and drop not thy word against the **house of Isaac**."

Israel was called the "house of Isaac." The Sakā/Scythians were the house of Isaac.

### Gimirri / Cimmerians

The Assyrians called these people "Gimirri." The Greeks rendered this as "Cimmerians" (Κιμμέριοι).

Possible Hebrew connections:
- **Gomer** — Hosea's wife, who symbolized unfaithful Israel (Hosea 1:3)
- **Gamar** (גמר) — "to complete, to finish" — Israel was "finished" as a nation

The Behistun Inscription equates Sakā and Gimirri — they were the same people called by different names.

### Iskuza

Assyrian records also mention "Iskuza" — another rendering of "Isaac."

- Iskuza = Scythians (Greek)
- Iskuza ≈ Isaac + territorial suffix

## Scythian Characteristics

### What Greek Historians Recorded

Herodotus and other Greek historians described the Scythians as:

1. **Warlike** — Skilled horsemen and archers
2. **Pastoral** — Herded cattle and sheep
3. **Nomadic at first** — Lived in wagons, later settled
4. **Monotheistic tendencies** — Worshipped a supreme sky god
5. **Tabernacle-like structures** — Used tent-shrines
6. **Genealogy-conscious** — Kept careful tribal records

These characteristics align with Israelite culture:
- Warlike: Israel was a warrior nation
- Pastoral: The patriarchs were shepherds
- Nomadic history: 40 years in the wilderness
- Monotheistic: Worship of Yahweh
- Tabernacle: The Tent of Meeting
- Genealogy: Central to Israelite identity

### Scythian Religion

While Herodotus mentions various Scythian deities, scholars note:
- A supreme god above all others (like El/Yahweh)
- Taboo against graven images in some tribes
- Sacrificial practices similar to Levitical law
- Purification rituals

A people in apostasy (which Israel was at the deportation) would have syncretized, but the underlying structure remained.

## The Migration Pattern

### From Scythians to Europeans

The Scythians did not remain in the steppes forever. Under pressure from eastern peoples (Sarmatians, Huns), they migrated westward:

**Timeline:**
- 700-300 BC: Scythians dominant in Black Sea region
- 300 BC - 100 AD: Sarmatians push Scythians westward
- 100-400 AD: Gothic and Germanic tribes emerge
- 400-600 AD: Germanic migrations into the Roman Empire

**The Peoples:**

| Scythian Group | Became | Settled |
|----------------|--------|---------|
| Western Scythians | Goths | Germany, Spain, Italy |
| Royal Scythians | Ruling dynasties | Various |
| Sacae of Central Asia | Saxons | England, Germany |
| Various tribes | Franks, Vandals, Lombards | Western Europe |

### The Saxon Connection

The Saxons specifically are often linked to the Sacae:
- Saxon = Sac's sons = Sons of Isaac
- Saxons invaded Britain → Anglo-Saxons
- Saxon territories in Germany → Saxony

## Scriptural Confirmation

### Colossians 3:11

> "Where there is neither Greek nor Jew, circumcision nor uncircumcision, Barbarian, **Scythian**, bond nor free: but Christ is all, and in all."

Paul mentions Scythians alongside Greeks and Jews — as people who could be "in Christ." Why would Paul specifically mention Scythians unless they were relevant to his Israelite audience?

If Scythians were simply random pagans, why single them out? Because Paul knew the Scythians were lost Israelites — and they, too, could be reconciled through Christ.

## Archaeological Evidence

### Scythian Artifacts

Archaeological finds in Scythian burial mounds (kurgans) reveal:
- Advanced metalworking (cf. Israelite craftsmen)
- Animal art style (cf. Israelite animal symbolism)
- Burial practices with some Semitic parallels
- Trade goods from the Near East

### DNA Studies

Modern genetic studies show:
- European populations carry Middle Eastern ancestry
- R1b haplogroup (common in Western Europe) has ancient Near Eastern connections
- Migration patterns match the historical record

## Synthesis

The Scythian-Israelite connection is supported by:

1. **Timing** — Scythians appear exactly when Israel was deported
2. **Location** — Scythians appear where the deportees were placed
3. **Names** — Sakā = Isaac, Gimirri = Israelites
4. **Culture** — Pastoral, tribal, religious parallels
5. **Migration** — Scythians became the European peoples
6. **Scripture** — Paul mentions Scythians as potential recipients of grace

The Scythians were the deported Israelites. The Europeans descended from the Scythians. Therefore, Europeans are descended from Israel.
