
# Proof 42: Genesis 6 — The Corruption of All Flesh

## The Claim

The Flood came because "all flesh had corrupted his way upon the earth." This corruption was genetic — the mixing of angelic and human seed produced Nephilim and tainted humanity. Noah was saved because his lineage was uncorrupted.

---

## The Text

### Genesis 6:5-7

> "And GOD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. And it repented the LORD that he had made man on the earth... And the LORD said, I will destroy man whom I have created."

### Genesis 6:11-13

> "The earth also was **corrupt** before God, and the earth was filled with violence. And God looked upon the earth, and, behold, it was corrupt; for **all flesh had corrupted his way upon the earth**. And God said unto Noah, The end of all flesh is come before me."

---

## "Corrupt" = Genetic Corruption

### The Hebrew Word

"Corrupt" (Hebrew: *shachath*) means:
- To destroy
- To mar
- To spoil
- To corrupt

It's used for physical destruction/corruption, not just moral failing.

### "All Flesh"

"All flesh had corrupted **his way**" — the corruption was in the flesh itself, not just behavior.

This suggests:
- Genetic corruption
- The mingling of species/kinds
- The Nephilim pollution spreading

---

## Noah's Distinction

### Genesis 6:8-9

> "But **Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD**... Noah was a just man and **perfect in his generations**, and Noah walked with God."

### "Perfect in His Generations"

"Perfect" (Hebrew: *tamim*):
- Unblemished
- Without defect
- Complete, whole

Used for:
- Sacrificial animals (must be without blemish)
- Physical perfection

"In his generations" (*toledoth* — genealogy, lineage)

Noah was *tamim* in his *toledoth* — unblemished in his lineage. His genetics were uncorrupted.

### Why Noah Was Saved

1. He was righteous (moral)
2. He was *tamim* in his lineage (genetic)
3. He alone (with family) was suitable to restart humanity

---

## The Extent of Corruption

### How Bad Was It?

> **Genesis 6:5** — "Every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was **only evil continually**."

> **Genesis 6:12** — "**All flesh** had corrupted his way."

This was not merely human sinfulness (which continues after the Flood). This was corruption of the species itself through Nephilim mixing.

### Only Noah Left

If the corruption was only moral, there would likely be other righteous people. But if the corruption was genetic, then only Noah's family remained uncorrupted — making them the only viable option.

---

## The Purpose of the Flood

### To Preserve the Seed

The Flood preserved:
- The Adamic line through Noah
- The promise of the Messiah (through Noah → Shem → Abraham → Christ)
- Genetic purity for the eventual Israelite nation

### To Destroy the Corrupted

The Flood destroyed:
- The Nephilim
- Humans corrupted with Nephilim genetics
- The "all flesh" that had been ruined

---

## After the Flood

### The Corruption Returned

> **Genesis 6:4** — "There were giants in the earth in those days; **and also after that**."

The Nephilim bloodline survived. How?

**Possible explanations:**
1. Ham's wife or another carried the genetics
2. A second incursion of fallen angels
3. Some Nephilim survived outside the Flood zone

### Giants in Canaan

The post-Flood Nephilim descendants:
- Anakim (Numbers 13:33)
- Rephaim (Genesis 14:5)
- Various giant tribes

God commanded Israel to destroy these peoples — continuing the cleansing the Flood began.

---

## Christ and Noah

### Matthew 24:37-39

> "But **as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be**. For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage... And knew not until the flood came, and took them all away; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be."

The end times will be like Noah's day:
- Corruption spreading
- Mixed "marriages" (possibly genetic corruption)
- Judgment coming suddenly

---

## Synthesis

Genesis 6 proves:

1. **Corruption was genetic** — "All flesh" corrupted, not just behavior
2. **Noah was genetically pure** — *Tamim* in his generations
3. **The Flood was cleansing** — Removing corrupted genetics
4. **Some survived** — Giants after the Flood
5. **Pattern continues** — End times like Noah's day

Understanding Genesis 6 reveals why lineage matters — why Israel must remain separate — why certain peoples cannot be saved.
