
# Proof 38: The Edomite Infiltration of Judea

## The Claim

Around 150 BC, the Judean Hasmoneans conquered Edom and forcibly converted the Edomites, who then became "Judeans" (Jews). By Christ's time, Edomites held key positions of power, including the throne (Herod) and the priesthood.

---

## Historical Background

### The Hasmonean Conquest

**Josephus, Antiquities of the Jews 13.9.1:**
> "Hyrcanus took also Dora and Marissa, cities of Idumea [Edom], and subdued all the Idumeans; and **permitted them to stay in that country, if they would circumcise their genitals, and make use of the laws of the Jews**; and they were so desirous of living in the country of their forefathers, that **they submitted to the use of circumcision, and of the rest of the Jewish ways of living**; at which time therefore this befell them, that **they were hereafter no other than Jews**."

Key points:
- John Hyrcanus I conquered Idumea (~125 BC)
- Edomites were forcibly circumcised
- They adopted Jewish customs
- They were "no other than Jews": legally indistinguishable

### The Problem

This forced conversion:
- Made Edomites into "Jews" by religion
- Did not change their ancestry
- Introduced Esau's seed into Judah
- Fulfilled the prophecy: Esau would "break his yoke" (Genesis 27:40)

---

## Herod the Edomite

### Herod's Ancestry

**Josephus, Antiquities 14.1.3:**
> "Antipater was an **Idumean** by birth."

Antipater was Herod's father. Herod was an Edomite, not a true Israelite.

### Herod's Rise

Herod was appointed King of Judea by Rome (~40 BC). An Edomite ruled over Judah, Esau had gained dominion over Jacob.

### Herod's Character

Herod displayed classic Edomite traits:
- Violence (murdered his own family members)
- Paranoia (killed babies in Bethlehem)
- Worldliness (massive building projects to glorify himself)
- Opposition to the true King (tried to kill Christ)

---

## The Priesthood

### Pharisees and Sadducees

By Christ's time, the religious leadership was corrupted:

**The Sadducees:**
- Denied resurrection
- Hellenized (adopted Greek culture)
- Controlled the Temple
- Many were Edomite converts

**The Pharisees:**
- Developed oral traditions (later the Talmud)
- Added to the law
- Some were true Israelites, some were Edomites
- Christ distinguished between them

---

## Christ's Recognition

### Not Abraham's True Children

> **John 8:37**: "I know that ye are Abraham's seed; but ye seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you."

Christ acknowledged they were "Abraham's seed", but through Esau (who was also Abraham's descendant), not through Jacob.

> **John 8:39**: "They answered and said unto him, Abraham is our father. Jesus saith unto them, **If ye were Abraham's children, ye would do the works of Abraham**."

Being Abraham's "seed" (physical descent through Esau) is different from being Abraham's "children" (spiritual heirs through Jacob).

### Children of the Devil

> **John 8:44**: "**Ye are of your father the devil**, and the lusts of your father ye will do. He was a **murderer from the beginning**."

Christ identified these particular religious leaders as:
- Children of the devil
- From a murderer "from the beginning" (Cain or Satan)
- Not true children of Abraham in the covenant sense

---

## "Jews" Who Are Not

### Revelation's Warning

> **Revelation 2:9**: "I know the blasphemy of them which **say they are Jews, and are not**, but are the **synagogue of Satan**."

> **Revelation 3:9**: "Behold, I will make them of the **synagogue of Satan, which say they are Jews, and are not, but do lie**; behold, I will make them to come and worship before thy feet."

Some claimed to be "Jews" (Judeans) but were not:
- They were lying
- They were a "synagogue of Satan"
- They would eventually acknowledge the truth

This perfectly describes the Edomite converts who became "Jews" by religion but not by ancestry.

---

## The Transition

### True Israel Scattered

True Israelites were already scattering before Christ (deportations, diaspora). After 70 AD:
- Jerusalem destroyed
- Temple demolished
- True Judeans scattered further

### Edomites Remained

The Edomite-Jewish population:
- Continued in various lands
- Developed Talmudic Judaism
- Eventually centered in Europe (Ashkenazi) and Spain (Sephardi)
- Claimed Israel's identity

---

## The Implications

### Who Are the "Jews" Today?

Modern "Jews" are a mixed population including:
- Some true Judahites (rare)
- Edomite descendants (from Hasmonean conversions)
- Khazar converts (medieval)
- Various other converts

The religious and cultural "Jewishness" does not equal Israelite ancestry.

### True Israel

True Israel:
- Was scattered to Europe
- Became the Christian nations
- Lost awareness of their identity
- Are "blind" to who they are (Isaiah 29:10)

---

## Synthesis

The Edomite infiltration proves:

1. **Historical fact**: Josephus documents the forced conversion
2. **Herod's ancestry**: An Edomite ruled Judea
3. **Christ's recognition**: He identified His enemies' true lineage
4. **Revelation's warning**: "Jews who are not"
5. **Modern implications**: "Jews" ≠ Israelites

Understanding this infiltration is essential for understanding who is who in biblical and modern contexts.
