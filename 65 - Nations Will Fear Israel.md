
# Proof 65: The Nations Will Fear Israel

## The Claim

Scripture prophesies that nations will fear Israel and serve them. Historically, the world feared European powers. In the future, restored Israel will again be feared and honored as God's people.

---

## The Prophecies

### Deuteronomy 28:10

> "And all people of the earth shall see that thou art called by the name of the LORD; and **they shall be afraid of thee**."

### Isaiah 60:12

> "For the nation and kingdom that will not serve thee shall perish; yea, **those nations shall be utterly wasted**."

### Isaiah 49:22-23

> "Behold, I will lift up mine hand to the Gentiles [nations]... and they shall bring thy sons in their arms, and thy daughters shall be carried upon their shoulders. And **kings shall be thy nursing fathers, and their queens thy nursing mothers**: they shall bow down to thee with their face toward the earth, and lick up the dust of thy feet."

### Micah 7:16-17

> "The **nations shall see and be confounded** at all their might: they shall lay their hand upon their mouth, their ears shall be deaf. They shall lick the dust like a serpent, they shall move out of their holes like worms of the earth: **they shall be afraid of the LORD our God**, and shall fear because of thee."

---

## Historical Fulfillment

### European Dominance

From ~1500-1950, European nations:
- Dominated every continent
- Controlled global trade
- Projected military power worldwide
- Were feared by all other peoples

| Era | European Status |
|-----|-----------------|
| 1500-1800 | Colonial expansion, feared |
| 1800-1914 | Peak power, all nations deferred |
| 1914-1945 | World wars (but still dominant) |
| 1945-present | Declining, no longer feared |

The nations feared Europe when Europe was Christian.

---

## The Decline

### When Fear Ended

As Europe:
- Became secular/apostate
- Abandoned Christianity
- Accepted mass immigration
- Lost confidence

...the nations stopped fearing them.

This matches Deuteronomy 28's curses:

> **Deuteronomy 28:25**: "The LORD shall cause thee to be **smitten before thine enemies**."

> **Deuteronomy 28:43**: "The stranger that is within thee shall get up **above thee** very high."

---

## Future Restoration

### The Prophecy Continues

The full prophecy describes future dominance:

> **Isaiah 60:14**: "The sons also of them that afflicted thee shall come bending unto thee; and all they that despised thee shall bow themselves down at the soles of thy feet."

> **Isaiah 61:6**: "Ye shall eat the riches of the Gentiles [nations], and in their glory shall ye boast yourselves."

This awaits fulfillment when:
- Israel is restored
- Christ rules
- The nations submit

---

## Who Will Be Feared?

### True Israel

The people who will be feared:
- Are God's covenant people
- Have been scattered
- Will be regathered
- Will rule under Christ

This describes:
- Europeans (scattered Israel)
- Under Christ's rule
- In the millennial kingdom

### Not Modern Israel

The modern state of Israel:
- Is feared somewhat (nuclear weapons)
- But primarily through alliance with USA
- Not inherently dominant
- Not fulfilling the full prophecy

---

## The Pattern

### Faithfulness → Blessing → Feared

When Israel is faithful:
1. God blesses them
2. They prosper
3. They become powerful
4. Nations fear them

### Apostasy → Curse → Despised

When Israel rebels:
1. God curses them
2. They decline
3. They become weak
4. Nations overcome them

European history follows this pattern exactly.

---

## Synthesis

Nations fearing Israel proves:

1. **Prophesied**: Deuteronomy, Isaiah, Micah
2. **Fulfilled historically**: European dominance
3. **Lost through apostasy**: Modern decline
4. **Future restoration**: Under Christ
5. **Identifies Israel**: Europeans match the pattern

The nations feared European powers when they were faithful. They will fear restored Israel again under Christ.
