
# Proof 37: Prophecies About Esau's Descendants

## The Claim

Multiple prophets spoke specifically against Edom (Esau's descendants), revealing their fate and character. These prophecies help identify who Esau's descendants are today and what will happen to them.

---

## Obadiah: The Anti-Edom Prophet

### The Entire Book

Obadiah's short prophecy is entirely about Edom:

> **v.1**: "Thus saith the Lord GOD concerning **Edom**."

### Pride and Self-Deception

> **v.3**: "The **pride of thine heart hath deceived thee**, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high; that saith in his heart, **Who shall bring me down to the ground?**"

Edom's characteristics:
- Pride
- Self-deception
- False security
- Arrogance

### Betrayal of Israel

> **v.10-12**: "For thy **violence against thy brother Jacob** shame shall cover thee, and thou shalt be cut off for ever. In the day that thou stoodest on the other side, in the day that the strangers carried away captive his forces... even thou wast as one of them. But **thou shouldest not have looked on the day of thy brother** in the day that he became a stranger; neither shouldest thou have **rejoiced over the children of Judah** in the day of their destruction."

Edom:
- Committed violence against Jacob
- Sided with Israel's enemies
- Rejoiced at Israel's destruction
- Participated in their captivity

### Complete Destruction

> **v.18**: "And the house of Jacob shall be a fire, and the house of Joseph a flame, and **the house of Esau for stubble**, and they shall kindle in them, and devour them; and **there shall not be any remaining of the house of Esau**; for the LORD hath spoken it."

Edom's fate:
- Devoured by Jacob and Joseph
- No remnant
- Complete annihilation

---

## Malachi

### Eternal Hatred

> **Malachi 1:3-4**: "And **I hated Esau**, and laid his mountains and his heritage waste... they shall call them, **The border of wickedness**, and, **The people against whom the LORD hath indignation for ever**."

Edom is:
- Hated by Yahweh
- Called "the border of wickedness"
- Under eternal indignation

### They Build, God Tears Down

> **v.4**: "They shall build, but **I will throw down**."

Whatever Edom constructs, Yahweh will destroy. Their efforts are futile.

---

## Ezekiel 35

### "Mount Seir" = Edom

> **v.2-3**: "Son of man, set thy face against **mount Seir**, and prophesy against it, And say unto it, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, O mount Seir, I am against thee, and I will stretch out mine hand against thee, and I will make thee most desolate."

### Perpetual Hatred

> **v.5**: "Because thou hast had a **perpetual hatred**, and hast shed the blood of the children of Israel by the force of the sword in the time of their calamity."

Edom has "perpetual hatred", not temporary enmity, but eternal, unchanging hatred of Israel.

### Blood Pursuing Blood

> **v.6**: "Therefore, as I live, saith the Lord GOD, I will prepare thee unto blood, and **blood shall pursue thee**: sith thou hast not hated blood, even blood shall pursue thee."

Edom is violent and will be repaid with violence.

### Claiming Israel's Land

> **v.10**: "Because thou hast said, **These two nations and these two countries shall be mine, and we will possess it**; whereas the LORD was there."

Edom claims Israel and Judah's inheritance. They seek to possess what belongs to Jacob.

---

## Isaiah

### Judgment on Edom

> **Isaiah 34:5-6**: "For my sword shall be bathed in heaven: behold, it shall come down upon **Idumea** [Edom], and upon the people of my curse, to judgment. The sword of the LORD is filled with blood."

> **Isaiah 34:8**: "For it is the **day of the LORD's vengeance**, and the year of recompences for **the controversy of Zion**."

Edom is connected to "the controversy of Zion", the conflict over who possesses Zion.

### Edom = Bozrah

> **Isaiah 63:1**: "Who is this that cometh from **Edom**, with dyed garments from **Bozrah**? this that is glorious in his apparel, travelling in the greatness of his strength? I that speak in righteousness, mighty to save."

Christ returns from judgment on Edom, His garments stained with their blood.

---

## Jeremiah

### Desolation

> **Jeremiah 49:17-18**: "Also **Edom shall be a desolation**: every one that goeth by it shall be astonished... As in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and the neighbour cities thereof, saith the LORD, no man shall abide there."

Edom's destruction compared to Sodom, complete, permanent.

### Uncovering Esau

> **Jeremiah 49:10**: "But **I have made Esau bare, I have uncovered his secret places**, and he shall not be able to hide himself: his seed is spoiled, and his brethren, and his neighbours, and he is not."

Esau's hiding places will be uncovered, he cannot remain hidden forever.

---

## Amos

### Edom's Crimes

> **Amos 1:11**: "Thus saith the LORD; For three transgressions of **Edom**, and for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof; because he did **pursue his brother with the sword**, and did cast off all pity, and **his anger did tear perpetually**, and he **kept his wrath for ever**."

Edom's sins:
- Violence against brother Jacob
- No pity
- Perpetual anger
- Eternal wrath

## Modern Application

### Identifying Edom Today

Who today:
- Has perpetual hatred of Europeans/Christians?
- Rejoices at their decline?
- Claims their inheritance (land, institutions)?
- Builds but sees it torn down?
- Has pride and self-deception?
- Will be uncovered from hiding?

The prophecies describe a people that can be identified by their actions.

---

## Synthesis

Prophecies about Edom reveal:

1. **Character**: Pride, perpetual hatred, violence
2. **Actions**: Betrayed Israel, rejoiced at their fall
3. **Goals**: Claiming Israel's inheritance
4. **Fate**: Complete destruction, no remnant
5. **Timing**: Judgment connected to Christ's return

These prophecies help identify Esau's descendants today and assure Israel of their ultimate deliverance.
