
# Proof 9: Greeks and Romans Were Israelites

## The Claim

The Greeks and Romans descend from Israelite migrations. The Dorian Greeks invaded from the north (from Israelite migrations). Rome was founded by peoples from the East. Both peoples became Christian because they recognized their Shepherd's voice.

---

## The Greeks

### The Dorian Invasion

Around 1100-1000 BC, the Dorian Greeks invaded the Greek peninsula from the north. They:
- Overthrew the Mycenaean civilization
- Brought iron weapons and new customs
- Established Sparta and other Dorian cities
- Spoke a distinct Greek dialect

**Where did they come from?** The north — the same direction as later Scythian migrations.

### The Danaans

Homer's *Iliad* calls the Greeks "Danaans" (Δαναοί). This name appears repeatedly:
- Danaans = Greeks (in Homer)
- Dana = Tribe of Dan

The connection: Danites were seafarers (Judges 5:17: "why did Dan remain in ships?"). Dan had contact with the Mediterranean world through Phoenician trade. Danite settlements could have predated the main Dorian invasion.

**Note:** The Tuatha de Danann (Ireland), Danaans (Greece), and Danes (Denmark) all share the "Dan" root.

### Javan — The Biblical Greeks

> **Genesis 10:2-4** — "The sons of Japheth; Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and **Javan**... And the sons of Javan; Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim."

"Javan" is the Hebrew name for Greece (still used in modern Hebrew: יָוָן). The sons of Javan represent:
- Elishah — Possibly Aeolian Greeks
- Tarshish — Tarsus/Spain
- Kittim — Cyprus
- Dodanim — Dodona (Oracle of Zeus)

**Important:** Japheth's descendants (including Javan) mixed with and were absorbed by the Israelite migrations. The "Greeks" of the New Testament era included this mixed population — which is why the apostles wrote to them.

### Greek Culture and Israelite Parallels

| Greek Element | Israelite Parallel |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Olympic games | Appointed feasts, competitions |
| Oracles (prophets) | Israelite prophets |
| City-states with councils | Tribal governance |
| Emphasis on law | Torah-based society |
| Philosophy (wisdom) | Hebrew wisdom tradition |
| Drama (tragedy/comedy) | Psalms, prophetic narratives |

## The Romans

### Rome's Founding Legends

**The Wolf of Benjamin:**

> **Genesis 49:27** — "Benjamin shall ravin as a **wolf**: in the morning he shall devour the prey, and at night he shall divide the spoil."

Rome's founding legend: Romulus and Remus were suckled by a **wolf**. The wolf became Rome's symbol.

Benjamin was the only tribe fully loyal to Judah after the kingdom split. Benjamites may have migrated early, before the Assyrian deportations.

**The Trojan Connection:**

Roman tradition claimed descent from Trojan refugees (Aeneas). Troy was in Anatolia — the region where:
- Israelites were later deported
- Phoenician trade reached
- Early Dan/Israelite settlements may have existed

### The Apostle Paul — A Benjamite to Rome

> **Romans 11:1** — "For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the **tribe of Benjamin**."

> **Philippians 3:5** — "Of the stock of Israel, of the **tribe of Benjamin**, an Hebrew of the Hebrews."

Paul, a Benjamite, was called to minister to Rome. This makes sense if Romans had Benjamite ancestry — Paul was ministering to his own tribe.

### Roman Law and Mosaic Law

Roman law developed parallel structures to Mosaic law:

| Roman Law | Mosaic Law |
|-----------|------------|
| Twelve Tables (foundation) | Ten Commandments + statutes |
| Paterfamilias (family headship) | Patriarchal authority |
| Property rights | Levitical property laws |
| Contract law | Covenant structure |
| Punishment fits crime | Eye for eye |

This doesn't require direct copying — it suggests a common origin in the moral law given to Adam and Noah.

## The New Testament Context

### Greeks in the New Testament

The New Testament frequently mentions "Greeks" who believed:

> **John 12:20-21** — "And there were certain **Greeks** among them that came up to worship at the feast... Sir, we would see Jesus."

> **Acts 17:4** — "And some of them believed... and of the devout **Greeks** a great multitude."

> **Acts 18:4** — "And he reasoned in the synagogue every sabbath, and persuaded the **Jews and the Greeks**."

These Greeks were drawn to the God of Israel before Christ came. Why? Because they were Israelites who had lost their national identity but retained a spiritual connection.

### Paul's Mission to the Greeks

Paul spent most of his ministry in Greek cities:
- Corinth, Ephesus, Philippi, Thessalonica, Athens

He wrote to them as fellow Israelites:

> **Romans 9:24** — "Even us, whom he hath called, not of the Jews only, but also of the **Gentiles** [nations]."

The Greek "ethnos" means "nations" — the nations of scattered Israel.

## Christianity's Spread

### Why Did Greeks and Romans Convert?

Christianity spread rapidly through the Greek and Roman world because:

1. **They recognized the Shepherd** — "My sheep hear my voice" (John 10:27)
2. **They had been prepared** — Greek philosophy sought the Logos; Rome sought justice
3. **They were Israelites** — The New Covenant was for Israel (Jeremiah 31:31)
4. **The apostles came to them** — Fulfilling Christ's command to find the lost sheep

### The Contrast

| People | Response to Christianity |
|--------|-------------------------|
| Greeks/Romans | Mass conversion |
| Persians | Limited, persecuted |
| Indians | Minimal |
| Chinese | None until modern era |
| Africans (except North) | None until colonization |
| Jews (Edomite-influenced) | Rejected Christ |

Only the European peoples — Greeks, Romans, and the Germanic tribes — embraced Christianity as nations.

## Synthesis

The Greeks and Romans were Israelites because:

1. **Migration patterns** — Dorian invasion from the north, Roman Trojan connection
2. **Tribal symbols** — Wolf of Benjamin in Rome, Dan in Danaans
3. **Cultural parallels** — Law, religion, wisdom traditions
4. **Paul's ministry** — A Benjamite to Rome, writing to kinsmen
5. **Conversion** — They recognized Christ and became Christian nations

The apostles went to the lost sheep of Israel. They went to Greece and Rome. Greece and Rome converted. Therefore, Greeks and Romans were Israelites.
