
# Proof 53: All Adamic Nations Ceased to Exist

## The Claim

The ancient nations descended from Adam, Egypt, Babylon, Assyria, Persia, Greece, Rome, have all ceased to exist as ethnic/national entities. Only the Israelites survived (as Europeans), preserved by God for His purposes.

---

## The Ancient Nations

### Genesis 10 The Table of Nations

Genesis 10 lists the descendants of Noah's three sons:
- **Shem**: Semites (including Israel)
- **Ham**: Egyptians, Canaanites, Cushites
- **Japheth**: Greeks, Medes, various European groups

These were all Adamic nations, descended from Noah, from Adam.

### Egypt

- Ancient Egyptians were a distinct people
- Conquered by Persia, Greece, Rome

- Modern Egyptians are largely Arab, not ancient Egyptian
- The ancient Egyptian race has ceased

### Babylon/Assyria

- Ancient Mesopotamian peoples
- Conquered repeatedly
- Arab and other invasions
- Modern Iraqis are not the ancient Babylonians
- The ancient race has ceased

### Persia

- Ancient Persians were Aryan/Japhetic
- Mixed with many peoples over centuries
- Modern Iranians are mixed

### Greece

- Ancient Greeks (Dorians, etc.) are debated
- If they were Israelites/Japhethites, their descendants are in modern Europe
- Modern Greece has mixed population
- The ancient Greeks as a distinct nation transformed

### Rome

- Ancient Romans likewise
- If they were Benjamites/Israelites, absorbed into European peoples
- The Roman nation ceased as Rome fell
- But the Roman people became Europeans

## Israel Survives

### Preserved Through Scattering

Unlike other ancient nations:
- Israel was scattered, not absorbed
- Israel maintained identity (though hidden)

- Israel continues in Europeans today

Other nations:
- Conquered and absorbed

Israel:
- Scattered by divine purpose
- Preserved by divine promise
- Multiplied as prophesied
