
# Proof 66: Daniel's Vision — The Stone Cut Without Hands

## The Claim

Daniel interpreted Nebuchadnezzar's dream of a statue destroyed by a stone "cut without hands." This stone became a great mountain filling the earth. The stone is Christ and His kingdom — Christian Europe — which destroyed pagan Rome and filled the earth.

---

## The Dream

### Daniel 2:31-35

> "Thou, O king, sawest, and behold a great image... This image's **head was of fine gold**, his **breast and his arms of silver**, his **belly and his thighs of brass**, His **legs of iron**, his **feet part of iron and part of clay**. Thou sawest till that a **stone was cut out without hands**, which **smote the image upon his feet** that were of iron and clay, and brake them to pieces... and the **stone that smote the image became a great mountain, and filled the whole earth**."

---

## The Interpretation

### Daniel 2:36-45

| Part | Metal | Kingdom |
|------|-------|---------|
| Head | Gold | Babylon |
| Chest/Arms | Silver | Medo-Persia |
| Belly/Thighs | Brass | Greece |
| Legs | Iron | Rome |
| Feet | Iron/Clay | Divided Rome |
| Stone | — | God's Kingdom |

> **v.44** — "And in the days of these kings shall **the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed**: and the kingdom shall not be left to other people, but it shall **break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms**, and **it shall stand for ever**."

---

## The Stone = Christ's Kingdom

### "Cut Without Hands"

The stone was "cut out without hands":
- Not human effort
- Divine origin
- Supernatural establishment

> **Daniel 2:45** — "The stone was cut out of the mountain **without hands**."

Christ's kingdom was established by God, not by human politics.

### The Strike

The stone struck the image on its feet — the divided Roman Empire. It didn't strike the head (Babylon) or chest (Persia) — it struck at the time of Rome's division.

---

## Historical Fulfillment

### The Fall of Rome

The Roman Empire fell to:
- Germanic tribes (Israelites)
- Goths, Vandals, Franks, etc.
- 5th-6th century AD

These tribes:
- Were Christianized
- Established Christian kingdoms
- Replaced pagan Rome

### The Mountain Filling the Earth

> **Daniel 2:35** — "The stone... became a **great mountain, and filled the whole earth**."

Christian Europe:
- Spread across the globe
- Colonized every continent
- Brought Christianity everywhere
- "Filled the earth" through expansion

No other entity matches this description.

---

## Why Europeans?

### The Identity of the Stone

If the stone is Christ's kingdom, and the kingdom:
- Destroyed Rome
- Filled the earth
- Stood forever (continues today)

...then the people of this kingdom are:
- The Germanic tribes who conquered Rome
- The Europeans who colonized the world
- Christians — those in Christ's kingdom

These are Israelites.

| Event | Date | Fulfillment |

| Germanic invasions | 400-600 AD | Stone strikes |
| Christian kingdoms | 500-1000 AD | Stone grows |

The timeline matches Daniel's vision perfectly.

## Still Standing

### Daniel 2:44

> "It shall **stand for ever**."

The kingdom:
- Has not been destroyed
- Continues in Christian civilization
- Will culminate in Christ's return

Though declining, the kingdom has not been "left to other people."

---

## Not Modern Israel

### The Test

Modern Israel doesn't match:
- Didn't destroy Rome
- Didn't fill the earth
- Isn't a kingdom "cut without hands"
- Didn't exist when Rome was struck

European Christendom matches. Modern Israel doesn't.

## Synthesis

Daniel's stone prophecy proves:

1. **Sequence correct** — Babylon → Persia → Greece → Rome
2. **Rome destroyed** — By Germanic (Israelite) tribes
3. **Kingdom filled earth** — European colonization
4. **Divine origin** — "Without hands" = not human effort
5. **Stands forever** — Continues today

The stone is Christ's kingdom, established through Christian Europe — the Israelites who conquered Rome and filled the earth.
