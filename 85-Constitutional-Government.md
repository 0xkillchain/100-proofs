
# Proof 85: Constitutional Government

## The Claim

Europeans developed constitutional government — limited government under law, with protected rights. This reflects Israel's covenant structure where even kings were bound by God's law.

---

## The Development

European constitutional tradition:
- Magna Carta (1215)
- English Bill of Rights (1689)
- U.S. Constitution (1787)
- Spread globally through European influence

---

## The Biblical Model

Israel's government was constitutional:
- Kings bound by Torah (Deuteronomy 17:18-20)
- Prophets held kings accountable
- Law above the ruler
- Covenant between God, king, and people

---

## Contrast

Other civilizations:
- Divine right of kings (unlimited)
- No higher law concept
- Subjects, not citizens
- No rights against the state

Only Europeans developed limited, constitutional government.

---

## Synthesis

Constitutional government reflects Israel's covenant structure — law above rulers, rights under God, accountable leadership.
