
# Proof 91: Medical Care

## The Claim

Europeans developed modern medicine and the hospital system. This reflects Israel's calling to preserve life and relieve suffering.

---

## European Contributions

- Hospitals (Christian invention)
- Medical schools
- Vaccines
- Germ theory
- Surgery advances
- Pharmaceuticals

Europeans transformed medicine from superstition to science.

---

## The Biblical Motivation

> **Matthew 25:36** — "I was sick, and ye visited me."

Christ healed. His followers built hospitals.

---

## Global Impact

European medicine:
- Extended life expectancy globally
- Eradicated diseases
- Reduced infant mortality
- Continues to advance

A blessing to all nations — as promised to Abraham's seed.

---

## Synthesis

Medical care reflects Israel's calling to bless all families of the earth — healing as service.
