
# Proof 86: Care for the Weak

## The Claim

European Christian civilization uniquely institutionalized care for the weak — orphans, widows, sick, poor, disabled. This reflects Israel's covenant obligations to protect the vulnerable.

---

## What Europeans Built

- **Hospitals** — Christian invention
- **Orphanages** — Church-founded
- **Almshouses** — For the poor
- **Asylums** — For the mentally ill (originally compassionate)
- **Schools** — For all children

No other civilization built these institutions organically.

---

## The Biblical Command

> **James 1:27** — "Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction."

> **Deuteronomy 10:18** — "He doth execute the judgment of the fatherless and widow."

Israel was commanded to care for the vulnerable.

---

## Synthesis

European care for the weak fulfills Israel's covenant calling — protecting orphans, widows, and the afflicted.
