
# Proof 87: The Work Ethic

## The Claim

The "Protestant work ethic" — viewing work as calling, diligence as virtue — reflects Israel's understanding of labor as service to God, not mere survival.

---

## The Ethic

European (especially Protestant) work ethic:
- Work as divine calling
- Diligence honors God
- Laziness is sin
- Prosperity through honest labor

This fueled European economic development.

---

## The Biblical Foundation

> **Colossians 3:23** — "And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men."

> **Proverbs 6:6** — "Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise."

> **2 Thessalonians 3:10** — "If any would not work, neither should he eat."

Israel's scriptures taught diligent labor.

---

## Contrast

Other cultures:
- Manual labor often despised
- Work for slaves, not nobles
- No concept of work as worship

Europeans uniquely dignified labor.

---

## Synthesis

The work ethic reflects Israel's biblical values — labor as service to God, diligence as righteousness.
