
# Proof 47: Peter's Warnings About Infiltrators

## The Claim

Peter warned the early churches about infiltrators — false teachers who had crept in, speaking heresies, denying the Lord, and leading believers astray. These were not merely mistaken Christians but enemies of a different nature.

---

## 2 Peter 2: The Warning

### False Teachers Among You

> **2 Peter 2:1** — "But there were **false prophets** also among the people, even as there shall be **false teachers among you**, who privily shall **bring in damnable heresies**, even denying the Lord that bought them, and bring upon themselves swift destruction."

Characteristics:
- False prophets/teachers
- Among the believers (infiltrated)
- Bring in heresies secretly (*pareisago* = smuggle in)
- Deny the Lord
- Destined for destruction

---

## Their Nature

### Not Just Mistaken

> **2 Peter 2:12** — "But these, as **natural brute beasts, made to be taken and destroyed**, speak evil of the things that they understand not; and shall utterly perish in their own corruption."

They are described as:
- "Natural brute beasts" — their nature is bestial
- "Made to be taken and destroyed" — created for destruction
- Will "utterly perish"

This is not describing deceived Christians but beings of a different nature.

### Spots and Blemishes

> **2 Peter 2:13** — "And shall receive the reward of unrighteousness, as they that count it pleasure to riot in the day time. **Spots they are and blemishes**, sporting themselves with their own deceivings while they feast with you."

They:
- "Feast with you" — participate in Christian fellowship
- Are "spots and blemishes" — corruptions, impurities
- Sport in their own deceptions

They are among the believers but are not of them.

---

## Their Actions

### Exploitation

> **2 Peter 2:3** — "And through covetousness shall they with **feigned words make merchandise of you**."

They use:
- Covetousness (greed)
- Feigned words (false, crafted speech)
- Make merchandise (exploit for profit)

### Corruption

> **2 Peter 2:18-19** — "For when they speak great swelling words of vanity, they allure through the lusts of the flesh... While they promise them **liberty**, they themselves are the **servants of corruption**."

Their strategy:
- Swelling, empty words
- Appeal to fleshly desires
- Promise "freedom" but deliver bondage

---

## The Comparison to Angels

### 2 Peter 2:4

> "For if **God spared not the angels that sinned**, but cast them down to hell, and delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved unto judgment."

Peter connects these infiltrators with:
- The angels who sinned (Genesis 6)
- Sodom and Gomorrah (v.6)
- The ancient world destroyed by Flood (v.5)

The pattern: creatures who corrupted themselves and faced divine judgment.

---

## Balaam — The Type

### 2 Peter 2:15-16

> "Which have forsaken the right way, and are gone astray, following the way of **Balaam** the son of Bosor, who **loved the wages of unrighteousness**."

Balaam:
- Was a prophet (had spiritual access)
- Loved money
- Taught Balak how to corrupt Israel (Numbers 31:16)
- Is a type of infiltrators who corrupt from within

---

## Returning to Corruption

### 2 Peter 2:20-22

> "For if after they have escaped the pollutions of the world through the knowledge of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, **they are again entangled therein, and overcome**, the latter end is worse with them than the beginning... But it is happened unto them according to the true proverb, **The dog is turned to his own vomit again**; and the sow that was washed to her wallowing in the mire."

They:
- Had some knowledge of Christ
- Returned to corruption
- Are compared to dogs and pigs — unclean animals

The comparison to unclean animals suggests their true nature.

---

## Who Are These Infiltrators?

### Characteristics

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Among believers | Feasting with them |
| Different nature | Natural brute beasts |
| Made for destruction | Created for this purpose |
| Corrupters | Bring in heresies |
| Exploiters | Make merchandise of believers |
| Children of cursing | 2 Peter 2:14 |

These are:
- Descendants of the serpent seed
- Edomite/Canaanite infiltrators
- Those who "say they are Jews and are not"

---

## Modern Application

### Infiltration Continues

Today's infiltrators:
- Enter Christian institutions
- Corrupt doctrine
- Exploit believers financially
- Deny core truths while claiming faith
- Are "spots and blemishes" in churches

Peter's warning remains relevant.

---

## Synthesis

Peter's warnings reveal:

1. **Infiltrators exist** — Among believers, not outside
2. **Different nature** — Brute beasts made for destruction
3. **Corruption is their goal** — Heresies, exploitation
4. **Connected to angelic sin** — Same pattern
5. **Judgment coming** — They will perish

Understanding infiltration helps identify false teachers and protect the flock.
