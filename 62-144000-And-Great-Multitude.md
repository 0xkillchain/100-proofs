
# Proof 62: The 144,000 and the Great Multitude

## The Claim

The 144,000 sealed Israelites and the "great multitude which no man could number" are both Israel — the 144,000 as a select group (firstfruits), and the multitude as the broader saved population of Israel throughout history.

---

## The 144,000

### Revelation 7:4

> "And I heard the number of them which were sealed: and there were sealed **an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel**."

### Revelation 14:1-5

> "And I looked, and, lo, **a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand**, having his Father's name written in their foreheads... These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which **follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth**. These were redeemed from among men, being the **firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb**."

Characteristics:
- From the twelve tribes
- Father's name on foreheads
- Virgins (spiritually pure)
- Follow the Lamb
- Firstfruits

---

## The Great Multitude

### Revelation 7:9-10

> "After this I beheld, and, lo, **a great multitude, which no man could number**, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues, stood before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands; And cried with a loud voice, saying, **Salvation to our God** which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb."

### Revelation 7:13-14

> "What are these which are arrayed in white robes? and whence came they?... These are they which **came out of great tribulation**, and have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb."

---

## Both Are Israel

### "All Nations"

> "Of **all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues**"

This sounds universal, but consider:
- Israel became "many nations" (Genesis 17:5)
- Israel was scattered among all nations
- The Greek *ethnos* (nations) doesn't require every nation on earth

The "great multitude" is from the **nations of Israel** — the scattered tribes.

### Abraham's Promise

> **Genesis 22:17** — "I will multiply thy seed as the stars of the heaven, and as the sand which is upon the sea shore."

An "innumerable multitude" was promised to Abraham's seed — Israel.

---

## The Relationship

### Two Groups, One People

| 144,000 | Great Multitude |
|---------|-----------------|
| Numbered (144,000) | Innumerable |
| Sealed before tribulation | Come out of tribulation |
| Firstfruits | Full harvest |
| On Mount Zion | Before the throne |
| From 12 tribes (explicit) | From "all nations" (Israel scattered) |

Both are Israelites:
- 144,000 = Select group (martyrs? leaders? first to be sealed?)
- Great multitude = Broader saved Israel

---

## Firstfruits and Harvest

### Agricultural Metaphor

In Israelite agriculture:
- **Firstfruits** = First portion, offered to God
- **Harvest** = The full crop

The 144,000 as "firstfruits" implies:
- They are the first offering
- A larger harvest follows
- Both are the same crop (Israel)

---

## From Great Tribulation

### Revelation 7:14

> "These are they which **came out of great tribulation**."

The great multitude:
- Endured tribulation
- Were purified through suffering
- Now stand before God

This describes Israel's experience — suffering, persecution, but ultimately preserved.

---

## The White Robes

### Revelation 7:13-14

> "These... have **washed their robes**, and made them white in the **blood of the Lamb**."

They are saved by:
- The Lamb's blood (Christ's sacrifice)
- Washing (cleansing from sin)
- White robes (righteousness)

This is Israel redeemed by their kinsman-redeemer.

---

## Not Universal Salvation

### Specific Group

The great multitude is not "everyone saved":
- They come out of tribulation (a specific experience)
- They are before the throne (after death/rapture)
- They serve God in His temple (Revelation 7:15)

They are the saved of Israel — those who accepted Christ.

---

## Synthesis

The 144,000 and great multitude prove:

1. **Both are Israel** — 12 tribes + nations of Israel
2. **Firstfruits and harvest** — Two aspects of same people
3. **Innumerable** — Fulfills promise to Abraham
4. **From tribulation** — Israel's suffering
5. **Redeemed by Christ** — Lamb's blood cleanses

The saved of Israel are both numbered (144,000 sealed) and innumerable (the great multitude).
