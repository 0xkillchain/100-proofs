
# Proof 41: The Nephilim and Fallen Angels

## The Claim

Fallen angels mated with human women, producing the Nephilim — giant hybrid beings. This corruption of the human race was a primary reason for the Flood. The Nephilim bloodline survived through various means and continues to oppose God's people.

---

## The Key Passage

### Genesis 6:1-4

> "And it came to pass, when men began to multiply on the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them, That **the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives** of all which they chose... There were **giants** [Nephilim] in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became **mighty men** which were of old, men of renown."

---

## "Sons of God" = Angels

### The Term

"Sons of God" (Hebrew: *bene elohim*) in the Old Testament refers to angels:

> **Job 1:6** — "Now there was a day when the **sons of God** came to present themselves before the LORD, and **Satan** came also among them."

> **Job 2:1** — "Again there was a day when the **sons of God** came to present themselves before the LORD."

> **Job 38:7** — "When the morning stars sang together, and all the **sons of God** shouted for joy?"

In Job, "sons of God" are clearly angels — supernatural beings in God's court.

### Why Not "Godly Men"?

Some claim "sons of God" means Seth's line. Problems:
- The term never means that elsewhere in the Old Testament
- Seth's line marrying Cain's line wouldn't produce giants
- The result (Nephilim) requires supernatural explanation
- Jude and 2 Peter confirm the angelic interpretation

---

## New Testament Confirmation

### Jude 6-7

> "And **the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation**, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day. Even as **Sodom and Gomorrha**, and the cities about them in like manner, **giving themselves over to fornication, and going after strange flesh**, are set forth for an example."

Key points:
- Angels left their proper place
- Their sin was like Sodom's — sexual ("strange flesh")
- They are now imprisoned awaiting judgment

### 2 Peter 2:4-5

> "For if **God spared not the angels that sinned**, but cast them down to hell [Tartarus], and delivered them into chains of darkness... And **spared not the old world**, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly."

Peter connects:
- Angels that sinned
- Chains of darkness
- The Flood

The sinning angels' crime was connected to the pre-Flood corruption.

---

## The Nephilim

### Who Were They?

"Nephilim" (נְפִילִים) = "fallen ones"

They were:
- Hybrid offspring of angels and humans
- Giants (Genesis 6:4, Numbers 13:33)
- "Mighty men" (*gibborim*)
- "Men of renown" (famous, legendary)

### After the Flood

> **Genesis 6:4** — "There were giants in the earth in those days; **and also after that**."

The Nephilim existed before the Flood and "after that" — they or their bloodline survived.

> **Numbers 13:33** — "And there we saw the **giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants**: and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers."

The Anakim in Canaan were Nephilim descendants. Israel was commanded to destroy them.

---

## Nephilim Tribes in Canaan

### The Giants

Multiple groups of giants existed:
- **Anakim** — Descendants of Anak (Numbers 13:33)
- **Rephaim** — "Shades" or giants (Genesis 14:5)
- **Emim** — "Terrible ones" (Deuteronomy 2:10-11)
- **Zamzummim** — Giants in Ammon (Deuteronomy 2:20)
- **Amorites** — Described as tall as cedars (Amos 2:9)

### Why Destroy Canaan?

God commanded Israel to utterly destroy the Canaanites. Why such severity?

The Canaanites were:
- Corrupted with Nephilim bloodline
- Irredeemable
- A threat to Israel's purity
- Under divine judgment

---

## How Did Nephilim Survive the Flood?

### Theories

1. **Ham's wife** — One of Noah's daughters-in-law carried Nephilim genetics
2. **Second incursion** — Angels sinned again after the Flood
3. **Not all imprisoned** — Some fallen angels remained free

The text says Nephilim existed "before the Flood" and "after" — the bloodline persisted somehow.

### The Corruption Spread

Through intermarriage:
- Nephilim → Canaanites
- Canaanites → Esau's wives
- Esau → Edomites
- Edomites → Infiltrated Judea

The corrupted bloodline wound through history.

---

## The Purpose of the Flood

### Genesis 6:9

> "**Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations**."

"Perfect in his generations" (Hebrew: *tamim* — unblemished) suggests genetic purity — Noah's lineage was uncorrupted by Nephilim.

The Flood was to:
- Preserve the Adamic line
- Destroy the corrupted Nephilim
- Start fresh with genetically pure Noah

---

## Synthesis

The Nephilim reality proves:

1. **Angels sinned** — Left their estate, mated with women
2. **Produced hybrids** — The Nephilim, giants
3. **Corrupted humanity** — Reason for the Flood
4. **Survived partially** — Giants in Canaan after Flood
5. **Connected to enemies** — Canaanites, Edomites carry this bloodline

Understanding the Nephilim explains why God commanded Israel to remain separate and why certain peoples cannot be redeemed.
