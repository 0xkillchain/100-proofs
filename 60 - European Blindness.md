
# Proof 60: European Blindness to Their Identity

## The Claim

Scripture prophesied that Israel would be blinded to their own identity. Europeans are the only people completely unaware they are Israelites, exactly as prophesied. This blindness itself is proof of their identity.

---

## The Prophecies

### Isaiah 29:10-12

> "For the LORD hath poured out upon you the **spirit of deep sleep**, and hath **closed your eyes**: the prophets and your rulers, the seers hath he covered. And the vision of all is become unto you as the words of a **book that is sealed**."

Israel would be:
- In deep sleep
- Eyes closed
- Unable to understand their own prophecies

### Romans 11:7-8

> "Israel hath not obtained that which he seeketh for; but the election hath obtained it, and **the rest were blinded** (According as it is written, God hath given them the **spirit of slumber, eyes that they should not see**, and ears that they should not hear;) unto this day."

Paul confirms:
- Israel is blinded
- This blindness is prophesied
- It continues "unto this day"

### Romans 11:25

> "For I would not, brethren, that ye should be ignorant of this mystery, lest ye should be wise in your own conceits; that **blindness in part is happened to Israel**, until the fulness of the Gentiles [nations] be come in."

The blindness:
- Is partial ("in part")
- Affects Israel
- Has a purpose and end point

---

## Who Is Blind?

### Europeans

Europeans today:
- Don't know they are Israelites
- Have forgotten their identity
- Think "Israel" means Jews
- Are unaware of the prophecies about themselves

This is precisely what was prophesied, Israel blind to their own identity.

### Jews

Jews:
- Know they claim to be Israel
- Are very conscious of their identity
- Are NOT blind about claiming the identity
- Therefore may NOT be the blinded Israel

---

## The Purpose of Blindness

### Romans 11:11

> "I say then, Have they stumbled that they should fall? God forbid: but rather through their fall salvation is come unto the Gentiles [nations], for to provoke them to jealousy."

The blindness served to:
- Scatter Israel to the nations
- Bring Christianity to Europe
- Build Christendom without ethnic consciousness
- Fulfill "all nations blessed"

### The Providential Hiding

If Israelites had known their identity:
- They might have remained ethnically isolated
- Christianity might not have spread as it did
- The "multitude of nations" might not have formed

God blinded them for His purposes.

---

## The Lifting of Blindness

### Romans 11:26

> "And so **all Israel shall be saved**: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob."

The blindness will end:
- All Israel saved
- The Deliverer comes
- Ungodliness removed

### Hosea 2:23

> "And I will say to them which were **not my people, Thou art my people**; and they shall say, Thou art my God."

Israel will be called "my people" again, the blindness removed.

Some Europeans are:
- Discovering their identity

- Awakening from slumber
- Recognizing the truth

This may be the beginning of the blindness lifting.

### The Timing

The blindness lasts until a certain fullness. When that's complete, eyes will open.

## The Paradox

### Blindness Proves Identity

The fact that Europeans:
- Are blind to their identity
- Match all prophetic marks
- Yet don't recognize themselves

...proves they are the blinded Israel prophesied in Scripture.

If they knew who they were, the prophecy of blindness wouldn't be fulfilled. Their blindness is itself the proof.

---

## Synthesis

European blindness proves:

1. **Prophesied blindness**: Isaiah, Paul predicted it
2. **Europeans match**: Unaware of identity
3. **Jews don't match**: Very aware of claimed identity
4. **Purpose served**: Christianity spread
5. **Will be lifted**: "All Israel shall be saved"

The blindness of Europeans to their Israelite identity is the final proof, they are the blinded Israel of prophecy.
