
# Proof 5: The Phoenicians Were Israelites

## The Claim

The Phoenicians who colonized the Mediterranean were Israelites. Tyre belonged to Israel, Phoenician colonies spread Israelite blood throughout Iberia, Gaul, and the British Isles, and Carthage was founded by Israelites from Tyre.

---

## Scriptural Evidence

### Tyre Belonged to Israel

> **Joshua 19:29 (Septuagint)**: The inheritance of Naphtali extended to "the fortified city of Tyre."

The Masoretic text obscures this, but the Septuagint (the Greek Old Testament used by Christ and the apostles) clearly places Tyre within Naphtali's territory.

> **2 Samuel 24:7**: "And came to the strong hold of **Tyre**, and to all the cities of the Hivites, and of the Canaanites: and they went out to the south of Judah, even to Beersheba."

David's census included Tyre as part of Israel's territory.

### Israelites in Tyre

> **Ezekiel 27:8-9**: "The inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad were thy mariners: thy wise men, O Tyrus, that were in thee, were thy pilots. The ancients of Gebal and the wise men thereof were in thee thy calkers."

> **Ezekiel 27:19**: "**Dan** also and **Javan** going to and fro occupied in thy fairs."

Dan and Javan (Ionians/Greeks) traded with Tyre. The tribe of Dan was actively involved in Phoenician commerce.

### Solomon and Tyre

> **1 Kings 5:1-12**: Solomon and Hiram of Tyre were allies. Hiram provided materials for the Temple, and Solomon gave Hiram cities in Galilee.

> **1 Kings 9:26-28**: "And king Solomon made a navy of ships... And Hiram sent in the navy his servants, shipmen that had knowledge of the sea, with the servants of Solomon."

Israelites and "Phoenicians" sailed together as one fleet. This was a joint Israelite venture, not a foreign alliance.

---

## Historical Evidence

### Who Were the "Phoenicians"?

The term "Phoenician" is Greek (*Phoinike*, "purple," from their dye trade). The people themselves did not use this name. They were:

- **Canaanites** (in the ethnic sense) who had been displaced or absorbed by Israel
- **Israelites** from Dan, Asher, Naphtali, and Zebulun: the coastal tribes
- **Mixed Israelite-Tyrian** populations under Solomon's unified kingdom

The "Phoenician" cities of Tyre, Sidon, and Byblos were within or adjacent to Israelite territory.

### Phoenician Colonies

The Phoenicians established colonies throughout the Mediterranean:

| Colony | Modern Location | Founded |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Carthage | Tunisia | ~814 BC |
| Gades (Cadiz) | Spain | ~1100 BC |
| Tartessos | Spain | Ancient |
| Malta | Malta | ~800 BC |
| Sardinia | Italy | ~900 BC |
| Sicily settlements | Italy | Various |
| Marseilles region | France | ~600 BC |
| British tin trade | Britain | ~1000 BC |

These colonies predate or coincide with the Assyrian deportations, meaning Israelites were already spreading westward through Phoenician trade routes.

### Carthage Founded by Israelites

Carthage was founded by Dido (Elissa) from Tyre around 814 BC. If Tyre was Israelite territory, then Carthage was an Israelite colony.

Carthaginian religion included:
- Worship of "Baal" (a title, not always pagan: Solomon's Temple area was called "Baalah")
- Child sacrifice (which Israel fell into during apostasy)
- Semitic language closely related to Hebrew

The Punic (Carthaginian) language was a Semitic language virtually identical to Hebrew.

### The British Tin Trade

> **Ezekiel 27:12**: "**Tarshish** was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of all kind of riches; with silver, iron, **tin**, and lead, they traded in thy fairs."

Tarshish supplied tin. The ancient world's primary tin source was Cornwall, Britain. Phoenician/Israelite traders reached Britain for tin as early as 1000 BC.

The name "Britain" may derive from Hebrew:
- **B'rith** = Covenant
- **Ain** = Land
- "Covenant Land"

---

## The Tribe of Dan and Sea Trade

> **Judges 5:17**: "...and why did **Dan remain in ships**?"

Dan was a seafaring tribe. When the Assyrians came, many Danites would have escaped by sea, to the Phoenician colonies they already traded with.

The Tuatha de Danann ("Tribe of Dan") in Irish legend arrived by ship from the east, bringing advanced knowledge.

---

## Synthesis

The "Phoenicians" were not a separate race from Israel, they were:

1. **Coastal Israelites**: Dan, Asher, Naphtali, Zebulun
2. **United with Tyre**: Under Solomon's kingdom
3. **Spreading westward**: Through established trade colonies
4. **Predating the deportations**: Israelites in Spain, Britain, and North Africa before Assyria fell

When the Assyrian deportations happened, the land route brought Israelites through Anatolia to Europe. The sea route, through Phoenician colonies, brought Israelites to Iberia, Gaul, and Britain.

Both routes led to Europe.
