
# Proof 67: The Monarchy of Europe

## The Claim

European monarchies claim descent from ancient Israel, particularly through the line of Judah. The "sceptre" promised to Judah has remained in European royal families, fulfilling Jacob's prophecy.

---

## Jacob's Prophecy

### Genesis 49:10

> "The **sceptre shall not depart from Judah**, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be."

Judah was promised:
- The sceptre (royal authority)
- Continuity until Messiah ("Shiloh")
- Ongoing kingship

---

## The Line of David

### 2 Samuel 7:12-16

> "When thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee... I will establish the throne of his kingdom for ever... **thy throne shall be established for ever**."

David's throne was promised perpetuity — forever.

### Jeremiah 33:17

> "For thus saith the LORD; **David shall never want [lack] a man to sit upon the throne** of the house of Israel."

A descendant of David would always reign over Israel.

---

## The Transfer to Europe

### The Tea-Tephi Tradition

According to tradition:
- After Jerusalem fell to Babylon (586 BC)
- Jeremiah took the royal daughters
- Including Tea-Tephi (a princess of Judah)
- They traveled to Ireland
- Tea-Tephi married an Irish king

This transferred the Davidic line to Ireland, then to Scotland, then to England.

### The Stone of Scone

The Stone of Destiny (Stone of Scone):
- Traditionally Jacob's pillow (Genesis 28:18)
- Brought to Ireland, then Scotland
- Used in coronations of Scottish/British monarchs
- British monarchs crowned on it until recently

## Coronation Ceremonies

### Israelite Elements

European coronations included:
- Anointing with oil (like Israelite kings)

### Psalm 72 at Coronations

British coronations traditionally include Psalm 72 — a psalm about the ideal Davidic king.

## "The Sceptre"

### Never Departed

If Jacob's prophecy is true:
- The sceptre must remain with Judah
- Some descendant must rule
- Until "Shiloh" (Christ) comes

If Christ came and the sceptre had departed, the prophecy failed.

**Solution:** The sceptre transferred to European monarchies — Israelite (Judahite) kings ruling scattered Israel.

## The Davidic Covenant

### Forever Means Forever

> **Psalm 89:34-37** — "My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips. Once have I sworn by my holiness that I will not lie unto David. **His seed shall endure for ever**, and **his throne as the sun before me**."

The covenant is:
- Unbreakable
- David's seed endures
- Throne continues "as the sun"

This requires a continuing monarchy — which European royalty provides.
