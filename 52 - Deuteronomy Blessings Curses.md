
# Proof 52: The Blessings and Curses of Deuteronomy

## The Claim

Deuteronomy 28 lists specific blessings for obedience and curses for disobedience. These blessings and curses have applied only to the European peoples, confirming they are Israel.

---

## The Blessings (Deuteronomy 28:1-14)

### Promised Blessings

> **v.1**: "If thou shalt hearken diligently... the LORD thy God will **set thee on high above all nations of the earth**."

> **v.7**: "The LORD shall cause thine **enemies that rise up against thee to be smitten** before thy face."

> **v.10**: "And all people of the earth shall see that thou art called by the name of the LORD; and **they shall be afraid of thee**."

> **v.12**: "The LORD shall open unto thee his good treasure... **to bless all the work of thine hand**: and **thou shalt lend unto many nations, and thou shalt not borrow**."

> **v.13**: "And the LORD shall make thee **the head, and not the tail**; and thou shalt be **above only, and thou shalt not be beneath**."

### Summary of Blessings

| Blessing | Promised |
|----------|----------|
| Above all nations | Yes |

| Nations fear you | Yes |
